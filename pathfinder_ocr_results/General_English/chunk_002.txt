Interrogative Pronouns
The interrogative pronouns are used for making queries or
asking questions. The pronoun 'who', 'what' and 'which'
are used as interrogative pronouns.
Rules of Interrogative Pronouns
1. 'What' is used in broad sense, while 'Which' is used in
the specific sense.
-
e.g. What are you doing these days?
-
Which institute have you joined for coaching?
2. 'Which' is used in place of '‘who' and 'what' when we
are referring to a choice between two or among more
than two things or persons.
e.g.
-
Of the two brothers who / which is more
intelligent?
