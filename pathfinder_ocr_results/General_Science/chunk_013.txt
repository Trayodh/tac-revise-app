ACIDS, BASES AND SALTS
The substances which have sour taste and turn blue litmus
to red are called acids.
•
According to Arrhenius acids give hydrogen ion H+ in
an aqueous solution.
HCl (aq) -> H(aq) + Cl(aq)
According to Bronsted Lowry concept, acids donate
proton (hydrogen ion).
BASES
These substances which have bitter taste and turn red litmus
to blue are called bases.
•
According to Arrhenius, bases give hydroxyl ion (OH)
in aqueous solution. e.g. NaOH, KOH, CsOH,Mg(OH)2,
etc.,
NaOH (aq) → Na*(aq) + OH¯¯(aq)
