IMPORTANT DEFINITIONS

Term Definition
River A natural flowing body of water that flows towards an ocean, sea, or lake
Lake A body of fresh or saltwater that is surrounded by land
Wetland An area of land that is saturated with water, either permanently or seasonally
Tributary A river or stream that flows into a larger river or lake
Delta A landform that forms at the mouth of a river, where the river deposits sediment
Estuary A partially enclosed coastal body of water, where freshwater from rivers and streams mixes with saltwater from the ocean
Waterfall A natural flow of water that drops from a higher elevation to a lower elevation
Dam A barrier constructed across a river or stream to impound water
Reservoir A body of water that is stored behind a dam or other barrier
Canal A man-made waterway that connects two larger bodies of water
