### 1.1 CDS Examination: Foundational Concepts & Process

The Combined Defence Services (CDS) Examination is a crucial test for young, courageous individuals aspiring to join the Indian Armed Forces as officers. Conducted twice a year by UPSC (generally in February and August), it recruits for the Indian Military Academy (IMA), Naval Academy (NA), Air Force Academy (AFA), and Officers Training Academy (OTA).

**Eligibility Criteria Snapshot:**

*   **Nationality:** Indian Citizen, Subject of Bhutan/Nepal, Tibetan refugee (pre-1962), or Person of Indian origin migrated from specific countries (with eligibility certificate). Gorkha subjects of Nepal are exempt from eligibility certificate.
*   **Age, Sex & Marital Status:**
    *   **IMA/INA:** Unmarried Male, 18-23 years (specific date ranges apply).
    *   **AFA:** Unmarried Male, 19-23 years (specific date ranges apply; up to 26 for Commercial Pilot License holders).
    *   **OTA:** Male (married/unmarried) & Female (unmarried/issueless widows/divorcees not remarried), 18-24 years (specific date ranges apply).
*   **Educational Qualification:**
    *   **IMA/OTA:** Degree from a recognized university or equivalent.
    *   **Naval Academy:** Engineering degree from a recognized university.
    *   **Air Force Academy:** Degree from a recognized university (with Physics & Mathematics at 10+2 level) or Bachelor of Engineering.

**Examination Pattern:**

The selection process comprises two stages:
1.  **Written Test:**
    *   **For IMA, INA, AFA:**
        *   English: 2 hours, 120 questions, 100 marks
        *   General Knowledge: 2 hours, 120 questions, 100 marks
        *   Elementary Mathematics: 2 hours, 100 questions, 100 marks
    *   **For OTA:** Only English and General Knowledge papers (200 marks total).
    *   **Negative Marking:** One-third of the marks will be deducted for each wrong answer.
2.  **Service Selection Board (SSB) Interview:**
    *   **Stage I:** Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR) tests + Picture Perception & Description Test (PP & DT).
    *   **Stage II:** Interview, Group Testing Officer (GTO) tasks, Psychology Tests, and Conference (conducted over 4 days).

    **Solution:**

    *   Therefore, `Sum(xi) - n * x_bar = 0`.
    *   So, the algebraic sum of the deviations from the mean is always 0.
