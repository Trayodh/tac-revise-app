### III. History: Ancient, Medieval & Modern India

*   **Gandhara School of Art:**
    *   **Flourished:** 1st Century BCE to 4th Century BCE.
    *   **Areas:** Afghanistan and present North-Western India.
    *   **Materials:** Initially blue schist and green phyllite. Stucco used increasingly after the 3rd Century BCE, eventually replacing stone as the main material.
    *   **Style:** Associated with Greco-Roman art.
    *   **Theme:** Main themes derived from Mahayana Buddhism.
*   **Gupta Coins:**
    *   **Dynasty Reign:** 319 to 467 CE (covered much of Indian subcontinent).
    *   **Gold Coins:** Issued in large numbers, known as 'Dinars'.
    *   **Silver Coins:** Issued by Chandragupta II, Kumaragupta I, Skandagupta, and Budhagupta.
    *   **General Features:** Usually minted in gold and silver. Obverse carved with images of kings, reverse with deities. Also depicted socio-political events (e.g., marriages, Ashvamedha Yagya).
    *   **Incorrect Statement:** Largest number of coins issued were NOT of copper.
*   **"The Philosophy of the Bomb":**
    *   **Author:** Bhagwati Charan Vohra.
    *   **Association:** Revolutionary freedom fighter, associated with Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA).
    *   **Death:** May 28, 1930, in Lahore while testing a bomb.
*   **Indian National Congress - Karachi Session (1931):**
    *   **Key Resolution:** Passed resolutions on Fundamental Rights (FR) and Economic Policy.
*   **Revolt of 1857:**
    *   **Beginning:** May 10, 1857, at Meerut (sepoy mutiny).
    *   **Causes:** Political, economic, and socio-religious activities of the British government.
    *   **Centers of Revolt:** Delhi, Kanpur, Ayodhya.
    *   **NOT a Center:** Agra.
*   **Azamgarh Proclamation (1857):**
    *   **Issued By:** Feroz Shah (grandson of the Mughal Emperor).
    *   **Context:** Declaration by rebels of 1857, fighting British forces in the Awadh region (Uttar Pradesh).
    *   **Nature:** A manifesto outlining rebels' aims.
    *   **Appeal:** Called for cooperation between Hindus and Muslims against the British.
*   **Ibn Batuta:**
    *   **Origin:** Moroccan Traveller.
    *   **Arrival in India:** AD 1334.
    *   **Envoy to China:** Sent by Delhi Sultan Muhammad bin Tughluq (AD 1324-51).
    *   **Writings:** Wrote about the history of the Tughlaq dynasty.
*   **Al-Biruni's Kitab-ul-Hind:**
    *   **Language:** Arabic.
    *   **Arrival in India:** Came with Mahmud Ghazni in 11th Century AD, lived for many years.
    *   **Learning:** Learnt Sanskrit.
    *   **Content:** Described socio-economic, political, religious, and economic conditions of India at that time.

### IV. Indian Polity: Parliament & Parties

*   **Privileges of Members of Parliament (MPs):**
    *   **Purpose:** To enable MPs to carry out duties without interference.
    *   **Types:** Collective and Individual.
    *   **Individual Privileges:**
        *   Immunity from arrest during Parliament session (available in **civil cases only**).
        *   MP may refuse to appear in court or present evidence during a Parliamentary session.
        *   No member is liable to any court proceeding for anything said or any vote given in Parliament or its committees.
    *   **Constitutional Context:**
        *   NOT fettered by Article 19(1)(a) (Freedom of Speech).
        *   MUST be read subject to Articles 20-22 (Protection in respect of conviction for offences, protection of life and personal liberty, protection against arrest and detention) and Article 32 (Right to Constitutional Remedies).
        *   **Does NOT cover:** Freedom of speech in his/her private or personal capacity.
        *   **Immunity:** Available in relation to civil prosecution, but NOT criminal prosecution.
*   **Appointment of Members of Parliamentary Committees:**
    *   **Method:** Members are appointed or elected on a motion made and adopted, OR nominated by the Speaker of the Lok Sabha or the Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
    *   **Secretariat:** Provided by the Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha secretariat.
    *   **Types of Committees (Indian Constitution):**
        *   **Standing Committees:** Permanent, constituted for a fixed tenure.
        *   **Ad Hoc Committees:** Appointed for a specific purpose, cease to exist after completing the task and submitting their report.
*   **Classification of Political Parties by Election Commission of India (ECI):**
    *   **Classified Categories:** National Parties, State Recognised Parties.
    *   **NOT a Classified Category:** Regional Parties (this is a common term but not an ECI classification category distinct from State Recognized). Registered Parties is a broader term, encompassing all parties registered with ECI, whether national, state, or unrecognised.

    *   *Mnemonic:* "GBC" for Fluvial (Gorge, Braids, Canyon); "CANE" for Glacial (Cirque, Aretes, Nunatak, Esker).

    *   **Dominant Phase:** Stucco completely replaced stone as the main material.
*   **Key Associations:** Greco-Roman influence, Mahayana Buddhism themes.

#### 6. Parliamentary Privileges: Scope and Limitations

*   **Core Principle:** MPs need freedom to speak and act without fear for effective legislative function.
*   **Key Distinctions:**
    *   **Immunity from Arrest:** ONLY in civil cases, during session.
    *   **Freedom of Speech in Parliament:** Absolute, protected from court proceedings.
    *   **Freedom of Speech OUTSIDE Parliament (Private Capacity):** NOT covered by parliamentary privileges.
    *   **Constitutional Limits:** Privileges are NOT absolute; they are subject to Articles 20-22 and 32.
    *   *Common Misconception:* Immunity extends to criminal cases – this is FALSE.
