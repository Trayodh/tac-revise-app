## Key Concepts
Some key concepts in Sequence and Series include:

*   **Arithmetic Sequences**: A sequence in which each term is obtained by adding a fixed constant to the previous term.
*   **Geometric Sequences**: A sequence in which each term is obtained by multiplying the previous term by a fixed constant.
*   **Harmonic Sequences**: A sequence in which each term is the reciprocal of the corresponding term in an arithmetic sequence.
