### I. Lines and Angles: Foundational Concepts

#### A. Basic Geometric Terms

*   **Point:** A figure with no measurable length, breadth, or height. Infinitesimal.
*   **Line Segment (PQ):** Straight path between two points P and Q. Has definite length and two endpoints.
*   **Line (PQ↔):** A line segment extended endlessly in both directions. Infinite points, no endpoints, no definite length.
*   **Ray (PQ→):** Extends indefinitely in one direction from an initial point P. No definite length.
*   **Collinear Points:** Three or more points lying on a single straight line.
*   **Non-collinear Points:** Three or more points not lying on a single straight line.
*   **Intersecting Lines:** Two lines sharing a common point (point of intersection).
*   **Concurrent Lines:** Three or more lines intersecting at the same point (point of concurrence).
*   **Parallel Lines (l || m):** Two lines in the same plane that never intersect, even when extended.

#### B. Types of Angles

An angle is formed by two rays sharing a common initial point (vertex).

1.  **Acute Angle:** 0° < Angle < 90°
2.  **Right Angle:** Angle = 90°
3.  **Obtuse Angle:** 90° < Angle < 180°
4.  **Straight Angle:** Angle = 180° (forms a straight line)
5.  **Reflex Angle:** 180° < Angle < 360°
6.  **Complete Angle:** Angle = 360° (full circle)

#### C. Pairs of Angles

*   **Complementary Angles:** Two angles whose sum is 90°. (Complement of x is 90° - x).
*   **Supplementary Angles:** Two angles whose sum is 180°. (Supplement of x is 180° - x).
*   **Angle Bisector:** A ray that divides an angle into two equal parts.
*   **Adjacent Angles:**
    *   Common vertex.
    *   Common arm.
    *   Non-common arms on either side of the common arm.
*   **Linear Pair of Angles:** Two adjacent angles whose non-common arms form a straight line. Their sum is 180°.
*   **Vertically Opposite Angles:** Formed when two lines intersect. Pairs of angles opposite each other are equal. (e.g., ∠POS = ∠ROQ, ∠POR = ∠SOQ).
    *   *Key Property:* Sum of angles around a point is 360°.

#### D. Angles Formed by a Transversal Intersecting Parallel Lines (l || m, n is transversal)

*   **Corresponding Angles:** Pairs are equal (e.g., ∠1 = ∠5, ∠2 = ∠6, ∠4 = ∠8, ∠3 = ∠7).
*   **Alternate Interior Angles:** Pairs are equal (e.g., ∠3 = ∠5, ∠4 = ∠6).
*   **Alternate Exterior Angles:** Pairs are equal (e.g., ∠1 = ∠7, ∠2 = ∠8).
*   **Interior Angles on the Same Side of Transversal (Consecutive Interior Angles):** Sum is 180° (e.g., ∠3 + ∠6 = 180°, ∠4 + ∠5 = 180°).
*   **Exterior Angles on the Same Side of Transversal (Consecutive Exterior Angles):** Sum is 180° (e.g., ∠1 + ∠8 = 180°, ∠2 + ∠7 = 180°).

### II. Height and Distance: Foundational Concepts

This section deals with applications of trigonometry, primarily using right-angled triangles.

#### A. Key Terms

*   **Line of Sight:** The line from the eye of an observer to the object being viewed.
*   **Angle of Elevation:** The angle formed by the line of sight with the horizontal, when the object is above the horizontal level.
*   **Angle of Depression:** The angle formed by the line of sight with the horizontal, when the object is below the horizontal level.

#### B. Trigonometric Ratios (in a Right-Angled Triangle)

Let θ be an acute angle:

*   **Sine (sin θ):** Opposite side / Hypotenuse
*   **Cosine (cos θ):** Adjacent side / Hypotenuse
*   **Tangent (tan θ):** Opposite side / Adjacent side

#### C. Common Angle Values

| Angle (θ) | sin θ          | cos θ          | tan θ          |
| :-------- | :------------- | :------------- | :------------- |
| 30°       | 1/2            | √3/2           | 1/√3           |
| 45°       | 1/√2           | 1/√2           | 1              |
| 60°       | √3/2           | 1/2            | √3             |
