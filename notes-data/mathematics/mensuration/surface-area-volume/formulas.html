# Volume Formulas Cube = a³ | Cuboid = l × b × h Sphere = 4/3 × π × r³ | Hemisphere = 2/3 × π × r³ Right Circular Cylinder = π × r² × h Right Circular Cone = 1/3 × π × r² × h Frustum of Cone = 1/3 × π × h × (R² + Rr + r²) # Total Surface Area Cube = 6a² | Cuboid = 2(lb + bh + hl) Sphere = 4πr² | Hemisphere (total) = 3πr² Cylinder = 2πr(r + h) | Cone = πr(r + l) [l = slant = √(r²+h²)] Frustum = π[R² + r² + l(R+r)] where l = √[h²+(R-r)²] # Lateral/Curved Surface Area Cylinder = 2πrh | Cone = πrl Sphere (hemisphere, curved) = 2πr²
