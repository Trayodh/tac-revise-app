Master these formulas once and they will never fail you."] Host: For painted cubes — the number of corner cubes is always 8, regardless of how many cuts you make. Corner cubes have 3 painted faces. Edge-middle cubes have 2 painted faces: there are 12 edges in a cube, and each edge (excluding corners) contributes n minus 2 cubes. Face-center cubes have 1 painted face: there are 6 faces, each contributing (n minus 2) squared cubes. [Audio: "For standard dice, the critical rule: opposite faces always sum to 7. One and six are opposite. Two and five are opposite. Three and four are opposite."] Host: When two dice views are given with a common face — if the common face appears in the same position (same direction facing you), the faces in the corresponding positions on the other sides are also opposite each other. When the common face appears in different positions, write the remaining faces clockwise from the common face in each view and match the positions.
