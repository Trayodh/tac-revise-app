️ UNESCO World Heritage Sites in India

India is home to 42 UNESCO World Heritage Sites (as of recent evaluations), safeguarding rich cultural and natural history.

1. Classification

  • Cultural Sites (34): Man-made historical monuments. E.g., Sanchi Stupa, Taj Mahal, Ajanta/Ellora Caves, Group of Monuments at Hampi, Sun Temple at Konark, Khajuraho temples, Hill Forts of Rajasthan.
  • Natural Sites (7): Pristine ecosystems. E.g., Kaziranga National Park (Assam - one-horned rhinos), Sundarbans National Park (Bengal - mangroves, tigers), Western Ghats, Keoladeo National Park (Rajasthan - migratory birds).
  • Mixed Site (1): **Khangchendzonga National Park** (Sikkim). Holds both cultural significance for local tribes and unique alpine biodiversity.
  • Recent Inclusions: **Santiniketan (West Bengal):** University town founded by Rabindranath Tagore.
  • Sacred Ensembles of the Hoysalas (Karnataka): Star-shaped temples at Belur, Halebidu, and Somanathapura.
