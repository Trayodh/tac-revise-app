️ Indian Philosophy & Festivals

The intellectual schools of thought and cultural celebrations across communities in India.

1. Six Orthodox Schools (Shad-Darshanas)

  • Samkhya (Sage Kapila): Oldest school. Dualism of Prakriti (matter) and Purusha (soul).
  • Yoga (Sage Patanjali): Focuses on physical and mental control as path to liberation.
  • Nyaya (Sage Gautama): Logicians. Stresses logical thinking and verification.
  • Vaisheshika (Sage Kanada): Introduced atom theory (*Anu*). Proved universe is made of physical elements.
  • Mimamsa (Sage Jaimini): Stresses the ritualistic teachings of the Vedas.
  • Vedanta (Sage Badarayana): Explores philosophical essence of Upanishads. Later divided into Advaita (monism of Adi Shankara) and Vishishtadvaita (Ramanuja).

2. Regional Festivals

  • Onam (Kerala): Harvest festival, celebrates King Mahabali's return. Famous for Snake Boat Races (Vallam Kali).
  • Pongal (Tamil Nadu): 4-day harvest festival.
  • Bihu (Assam): 3 festivals (Rongali, Kongali, Bhogali) marking agricultural cycles.
  • Hornbill Festival (Nagaland): "Festival of Festivals", showcasing rich tribal heritage.
