• British Expansion

1. Battles & Alliances

  • Battle of Plassey (1757): Robert Clive defeated Siraj-ud-Daulah (Nawab of Bengal) due to Mir Jafar's betrayal. Started British political footprint.
  • Battle of Buxar (1764): Hector Munro defeated combined forces of Mir Qasim, Shuja-ud-Daulah, and Shah Alam II. Led to **Treaty of Allahabad (1765)** granting Diwani rights (tax collection) of Bengal, Bihar, Odisha to the Company.
  • Subsidiary Alliance: Lord Wellesley (Hyderabad first to sign in 1798).
  • Doctrine of Lapse: Lord Dalhousie (Satara, Jhansi, Nagpur, Sambalpur annexed). Awadh annexed on grounds of misgovernment (1856).
