Socio-Religious Reform Movements

Movements in the 19th century aiming to reform social evils like caste, Sati, child marriage, and promote modern education.

1. Reform Organizations

  • Brahmo Samaj (1828): Founded by **Raja Ram Mohan Roy** (Father of Indian Renaissance). Opposed idol worship, caste rigidity, and Sati (which was banned in 1829 due to his efforts). Promoted monotheism. Later split into Adi Brahmo Samaj (Debendranath Tagore) and Brahmo Samaj of India (Keshab Chandra Sen).
  • Arya Samaj (1875): Founded by **Swami Dayanand Saraswati** at Bombay. Motto: **"Go Back to Vedas"**. Wrote *Satyarth Prakash*. Opposed priesthood and idolatry. Started *Shuddhi* movement to reconvert people to Hinduism. Set up DAV schools.
  • Satyashodhak Samaj (1873): Founded by **Jyotirao Phule** in Maharashtra. Dedicated to the upliftment of lower castes and women. Wrote *Gulamgiri* (Slavery) and *Saraswati*. His wife Savitribai Phule started India's first school for girls.
  • Ramakrishna Mission (1897): Founded by **Swami Vivekananda** in honor of his Guru Ramakrishna Paramahansa. Vivekananda preached Practical Vedanta and humanitarian service. Gave the historic speech at Chicago's Parliament of Religions (1893).
  • Aligarh Movement (1875): Founded by **Sir Syed Ahmed Khan**. Opposed blind adherence to religion, promoted scientific education for Muslims. Founded Muhammadan Anglo-Oriental College (now Aligarh Muslim University). Wrote *Tahzib-ul-Akhlaq*.
  • Prarthana Samaj (1867): Founded by Atmaram Pandurang in Bombay, later led by M.G. Ranade. Focused on inter-caste dining, widow remarriage, and women's education.
