The Revolt of 1857

Often called India's First War of Independence. A massive armed uprising against East India Company rule.

1. Causes of the Revolt

  • Political: Lord Dalhousie's Doctrine of Lapse and the annexation of Awadh (1856) on grounds of misgovernment alienated local rulers.
  • Economic: Heavy taxes on land and destruction of native handicrafts.
  • Social/Religious: Interference in social customs (abolishing Sati, Widow Remarriage Act) and fear of forced conversions by missionaries.
  • Military: Indian sepoys paid less than British, denied overseas allowance (*Batta*), and forced to travel across seas (violating caste rules).
  • Immediate: Introduction of the **Enfield Rifle** with grease paper cartridges containing pig and cow fat (offensive to Muslims and Hindus). **Mangal Pandey** of 34th NI shot his officer at Barrackpore (29 March 1857).

3. Outcomes

  • Passed the Government of India Act 1858, which transferred Indian administration from the East India Company to the British Crown.
  • Governor-General's title changed to Viceroy (Canning was first).
  • Abolished the Doctrine of Lapse. British stopped expanding territories, focusing on indirect control.
  • Army Reorganization: Ratio of European to Indian soldiers increased. Regiments restructured on caste/religion lines ("Martial Races" theory) to prevent unity.
