The Mughal Empire (1526 - 1857)

Established by Central Asian invaders, the Mughals built an extensive, culturally rich empire in India.

1. Rulers & Triumphs

  • Babur (1526-1530): Defeated Ibrahim Lodi in **1st Battle of Panipat (1526)** using gunpowder and *Tulughma* (flanking) tactics. Defeated Rana Sanga in Battle of Khanwa (1527). Wrote autobiography *Tuzuk-i-Baburi* in Turkish.
  • Humayun (1530-1556): Defeated and exiled by Afghan leader **Sher Shah Suri** (who built Grand Trunk Road, introduced rupee currency, and Dahsala land reforms, ruling 1540-45). Humayun recaptured Delhi in 1555 but died falling from his library stairs.
  • Akbar (1556-1605): Crowned at 13. defeated Hemu in **2nd Battle of Panipat (1556)** with help of guardian Bairam Khan. *Reforms:* Introduced **Mansabdari System** (grading officers by Zat/rank and Sawar/cavalry). **Todar Mal's Dahsala** (land revenue system based on 10-year average crop yields).
  • Religious Policy: Abolished Jizya tax on non-Muslims (1564). Built Ibadat Khana (House of Worship) at Fatehpur Sikri. Promulgated Din-i-Ilahi (divine monotheism) in 1852.
  • Jahangir (1605-1627): Paintings peaked during his reign. Executed 5th Sikh Guru Arjan Dev. Captain Hawkins and Thomas Roe visited his court.
  • Shah Jahan (1628-1658): Golden Age of Architecture. Built Taj Mahal, Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Peacock Throne.
  • Aurangzeb (Alamgir: 1658-1707): Imprisoned his father Shah Jahan. Executed 9th Sikh Guru Tegh Bahadur. Re-imposed Jizya tax (1679). Annexed Bijapur (1686) and Golconda (1687). Called "Zinda Pir" (living saint) due to simple lifestyle.
