️ The Vijayanagara Empire (1336 - 1646)

The supreme Hindu kingdom of Southern India, acting as a bulwark against northern Sultanate expansions.

1. History & Dynasties

  • Founded in 1336 by brothers Harihara I & Bukka I (Sangama Dynasty) on the southern bank of Tungabhadra River, inspired by sage Vidyaranya.
  • Four dynasties: Sangama, Saluva, Tuluva, and Aravidu.
  • Battle of Talikota (1565): Also known as Battle of Bannihatti. Combined forces of Deccan Sultanates (except Berar) defeated Vijayanagara army. Rama Raya was captured and executed. Capital Hampi was sacked.
  • Foreign Travellers: Nicolo de Conti (Italy, 1420), Abdur Razzaq (Persia, 1443), Domingo Paes (Portugal, 1520), Fernao Nuniz (Portugal, 1535).
