The Maratha Empire

The Marathas rose as a formidable power in the Deccan, challenging Mughal supremacy and eventually dominating North India.

1. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj (1627 - 1680)

  • Born at Shivneri Fort. Captured major forts (Torna, Pratapgad, Purandar). Escaped Aurangzeb's house arrest in Agra (1666). Coronation at Raigad (1674), taking the title Chhatrapati.
  • Administration: **Ashtapradhan** (council of 8 ministers): - *Peshwa:* Prime Minister. - *Amatya:* Finance Minister. - *Senapati:* Military commander. - *Sumant / Dabir:* Foreign relations.
  • Revenue System: - *Chauth:* 1/4th (25%) of land revenue paid by neighboring states to escape Maratha raids. - *Sardeshmukhi:* Additional 10% tax claimed by Shivaji as supreme ruler.

2. Peshwa Era (1713 - 1818)

  • Peshwas became the de-facto rulers of the empire, shifting capital to Pune. Balaji Vishwanath (1713-1720): Assisted Shahu in consolidating power.
  • Baji Rao I (1720-1740): Undefeated in 41 battles (Palhed, Bhopal). Captured Salsette and Bassein from Portuguese. Expanded empire to Delhi.
  • **Balaji Baji Rao (Nana Saheb: 1740-1761):** Maratha power peaked. **3rd Battle of Panipat (1761):** Maratha army under Sadashivrao Bhau was defeated by Afghan invader **Ahmad Shah Abdali**. This broke Maratha unity and halted expansion.
