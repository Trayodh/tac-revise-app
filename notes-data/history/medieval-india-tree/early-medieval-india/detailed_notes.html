Early Medieval India

An era of regional fragmentation marked by the Tripartite Struggle and the rise of Rajput kingdoms.

1. Tripartite Struggle (8th - 10th CE)

A century-long struggle between three major empires to control Kannauj (symbol of sovereignty in North India, situated in the fertile Gangetic plains):

  • The Palas of Bengal: Founded by Gopala. Dharmapala founded **Vikramashila University** and revived Nalanda. Followed Mahayana Buddhism.
  • The Gurjara-Pratiharas: Ruled Western India/Rajasthan. Mihira Bhoja was the greatest ruler.
  • The Rashtrakutas: Ruled Deccan. Founded by Dantidurga. **Krishna I** carved the monolithic **Kailash Temple at Ellora (Cave 16)**. Patronised Amoghavarsha (wrote *Kavirajamarga*).
