The Interwar Period (1919 - 1939)

The twenty years between the two World Wars, marked by economic collapse and the rise of totalitarian dictators.

1. Major Developments

  • League of Nations: Established in 1920 to maintain peace. Proved weak; could not halt invasions (e.g., Japan invading Manchuria 1931; Italy invading Ethiopia 1935).
  • Great Depression (1929): Began with the Wall Street stock market crash in USA. Led to worldwide decline in trade, high unemployment, and financial collapse, weakening democratic governments.
  • Rise of Dictators: **Fascism in Italy:** Benito Mussolini seized power in 1922. Promoted aggressive nationalism and corporate state.
  • Nazism in Germany: Adolf Hitler (Nazi Party) became Chancellor in 1933. Preached racial superiority, anti-Semitism, and rejected the Treaty of Versailles.
  • Militarism in Japan: Military leaders took control of government, expanding into China.
