International Institutions

Global bodies created to manage international relations, trade, finance, and security.

1. The United Nations (UN)

  • Established on 24 October 1945 (UN Day) to replace the League of Nations. Headquarters in New York.
  • Six Principal Organs: **General Assembly:** Deciding body where all member states have equal votes.
  • Security Council: Responsible for international peace. Has 15 members: 5 Permanent (P5 with veto power: USA, UK, France, Russia, China) and 10 non-permanent members elected for 2-year terms.
  • International Court of Justice (ICJ): Judicial body. Located at The Hague, Netherlands.
  • Secretariat: Executive branch, led by the Secretary-General.
  • Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC) and Trusteeship Council (suspended).

2. Financial & Trade Bodies

  • Bretton Woods Twins (1944): Created at the Bretton Woods Conference: - *International Monetary Fund (IMF):* Manages balance of payments and exchange rates. - *World Bank (IBRD):* Provides long-term development loans.
  • World Trade Organization (WTO): Established on 1 January 1995, replacing the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT). Regulates global trade.
