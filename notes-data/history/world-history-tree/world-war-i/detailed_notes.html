World War I (1914 - 1918)

A global war fought mainly in Europe, changing map boundaries and destroying empires.

1. Causes and Blocs

  • Causes: Imperialist competition, secret alliances, arms race, militarism, and rise of nationalism.
  • Immediate Cause: Assassination of **Archduke Franz Ferdinand** (heir to the Austro-Hungarian throne) in Sarajevo (Bosnia) on 28 June 1914 by Serbian nationalist Gavrilo Princip.
  • Alliances: - *Allied Powers:* Britain, France, Russia, Italy, Japan, and USA (entered 1917 after German submarines sank US ships). - *Central Powers:* Germany, Austria-Hungary, Ottoman Empire (Turkey), Bulgaria.

2. Outcomes

  • Ended with Germany's surrender on 11 Nov 1918. Signed the Treaty of Versailles (1919), placing the guilt of war on Germany, stripping its colonies, and imposing massive reparations.
  • Collpase of major empires: German, Austro-Hungarian, Russian (became USSR), and Ottoman (led to modern Turkey).
  • Created the League of Nations (1920) to prevent future wars (failed due to US absence and lack of military power).
