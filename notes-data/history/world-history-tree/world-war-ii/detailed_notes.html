World War II (1939 - 1945)

The most destructive conflict in human history, involving the majority of the world's nations.

1. Causes and Outbreak

  • Causes: Failure of the League of Nations, rise of fascism/nazism, policy of appeasement (Britain/France letting Hitler annex territory), and German dissatisfaction with Versailles.
  • Immediate Cause: Germany's invasion of **Poland** on 1 September 1939. Britain and France declared war on Germany.
  • Alliances: - *Axis Powers:* Germany (Hitler), Italy (Mussolini), Japan (Tojo). - *Allied Powers:* Britain (Churchill), USSR (Stalin), USA (Roosevelt / Truman), China.

2. Decisive Events & End

  • Pearl Harbor (7 Dec 1941): Japan attacked US naval base in Hawaii. Prompted USA to enter the war.
  • Battle of Stalingrad (1942-43): Turning point in Europe. Soviet Red Army defeated German forces.
  • D-Day (6 June 1944): Allied invasion of Normandy (France), liberating Western Europe.
  • The Holocaust: Systematic, state-sponsored genocide of 6 million Jews by Nazi Germany.
  • End: Germany surrendered in May 1945 (Hitler committed suicide). Japan surrendered after USA dropped atomic bombs: "Little Boy" on Hiroshima (6 August 1945) and "Fat Man" on Nagasaki (9 August 1945).
