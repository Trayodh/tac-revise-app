Dating Systems and Eras

Different calendars and dating conventions have been used throughout Indian history to record time.

1. General Terminology

  • BC (Before Christ) / BCE (Before Common Era): Counted backward. Year 500 BCE is older than 300 BCE.
  • AD (Anno Domini) / CE (Common Era): Counted forward from the birth of Christ.
