# Tense Structure Reference Simple Present: Subject + V₁(+s/es) | Negative: do/does not + V₁ Present Continuous: am/is/are + V₁+ing Present Perfect: has/have + V₃ (past participle) Present Perfect Continuous: has/have + been + V₁+ing + since/for Simple Past: Subject + V₂ | Negative: did not + V₁ Past Continuous: was/were + V₁+ing Past Perfect: had + V₃ (before another past action) Past Perfect Continuous: had + been + V₁+ing Simple Future: will/shall + V₁ Future Continuous: will/shall + be + V₁+ing Future Perfect: will + have + V₃ Future Perfect Continuous: will + have + been + V₁+ing # Key Signal Words Present: always, often, usually, every day Past: yesterday, ago, last year, in 2020 Perfect: already, just, yet, ever, never, since, for Future: tomorrow, next week, soon, by 2025
