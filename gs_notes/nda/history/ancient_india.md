## Chapter: Prehistoric India

### Subtopic: Early Human Presence

**Notes**
- Earliest hominid fossils (e.g., *Homo erectus*) dated ~1.5 Ma discovered at **Attirampur** (Uttar Pradesh).
- *Stone Age* phases: **Paleolithic**, **Mesolithic**, **Neolithic**.
- Tools: hand‑axes, microliths, polished stone axes.
- **Key Fact**: Mesolithic sites like **Bagor** (Rajasthan) show transition to settled life.

**Facts**
- First evidence of controlled use of fire ~800 ka.
- Rock‑shelter paintings at **Bhimbetka** (Madhya Pradesh) dated 10–4 ka, depicting hunting scenes.

## Chapter: Indus Valley Civilization (Harappan)

### Subtopic: Urban Planning & Architecture

**Notes**
- Two major phases: **Early Harappan** (c. 3300–2600  BCE) and **Mature Harappan** (c. 2600–1900  BCE).
- Cities: **Harappa**, **Mohenjo‑Daro**, **Dholavira**, **Rakhigarhi**.
- Grid‑like streets, baked‑brick houses, **Great Bath** (public water‑storage & ritual).
- Advanced **drainage** systems; uniform **brick size** (4:2:1 ratio).
- **Standardised weights** (≈ 1 kg) indicating trade & administration.

**Facts**
- Estimated population: 1–5 million across > 1,000 settlements.
- Script: undeciphered **Indus script** (~ 400  symbols).
- Economy: agriculture (wheat, barley, cotton), livestock, craft (bead‑making, metallurgy).

## Chapter: Vedic Age

### Subtopic: Early Vedic Period (Rig‑Vedic)

**Notes**
- Chronology: **c. 1500‑1200  BCE**.
- Society: **Tribal**, pastoral, **Patriarchal**.
- Major **Texts**: **Rig‑Veda**, **Sama‑Veda**, **Yajur‑Veda**, **Atharva‑Veda**.
- Political units: **Janapadas** – early kingdoms (e.g., **Kuru**, **Panchala**).
- Economy: cattle‑herding, barley, wheat, simple barter.
- Religion: **Brahmanas**, **Soma**, **Ritual sacrifices**.

**Facts**
- **Soma** – a ritual drink; identity still debated (possible *ephedra*).
- **Ashvamedha** – horse‑sacrifice, symbol of sovereign power.

**Diagrams**
- ![Early Vedic Society Pyramid](file:///C:/Users/Trayodh%20Khandalkar/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/63330b5a-af9a-4284-8f4b-225b7f5a6c88/gsnotes/assets/diagrams/vedicsociety.png)

**Memory Technique**
- **“R‑S‑K”** – *Rig, Soma, Kuru* – to recall key components of Early Vedic period.

**PYQ Analysis**
- 2021 asked: *“What was the primary occupation of early Vedic people?”* – Answer: **Pastoralism** (high‑yield).
- 2023: *“Identify the correct sequence of Vedic literature.”* – **Rig → Sama → Yajur → Atharva**.

**Common Mistakes**
- Confusing **Later Vedic** (c. 1000‑600  BCE) with **Early Vedic** – later period saw emergence of **Mahajanapadas** and iron‑tool usage.

**Revision Material**
- **Timeline**: 1500 BCE (Rig‑Veda) → 1200 BCE (Sama‑Veda) → 1000 BCE (Yajur‑Veda) → 800 BCE (Atharva‑Veda).

## Chapter: Mahajanapadas & Rise of Empires

### Subtopic: Sixteen Mahajanapadas (c. 600‑300  BCE)

**Notes**
- Classification: **Northern** (e.g., **Kuru**, **Panchala**, **Matsya**) and **Southern** (e.g., **Assaka**, **Kalinga**).
- Political system: **Monarchy** (most), **Republic** (e.g., **Vrijji**, **Mahā‑Māta‑Pañca**).
- Capitals: **Kapilavastu** (Koliya), **Pataliputra** (Magadha).
- Important **Conflicts**: **Buddha’s** encounters, **Jain** revolts, **Buddha’s** conversion of **Magadha**.

**Facts**
- **Magadha** evolved into **Mauryan Empire** under **Ajātasattu** → **Chandragupta Maurya**.
- **Taxation**: **Artha‑śāstra** mentions **land‑tax (bhaga)** & **trade‑tax (śulkā)**.

## Chapter: Mauryan Empire (c. 322‑185  BCE)

### Subtopic: Chandragupta Maurya & Administration

**Notes**
- Founder: **Chandragupta Maurya** (322 BCE) – aided by **Chanakya** (Kautilya).
- Capital: **Pataliputra** (modern Patna).
- Administration: **Centralised bureaucracy**, **Arthashastra** as policy manual.
- **Divisions**: **Rajyas** (provinces) → **Vishayas** (districts) → **Gram‑panchayats**.
- **Revenue**: Land‑tax (50 % of produce) – **‘Dharma‑sam’** system.

**Facts**
- ***Ashoka’s** edicts (330 +) inscribed on pillars & rocks in **Brahmi script**.
- **Mauryan army**: 600,000 infantry, 30,000 cavalry, war‑elephants.

**Memory Technique**
- **“C‑K‑P‑A‑R”** – *Chandragupta, Kautilya, Pataliputra, Arthashastra, Rajyas*.

**PYQ Analysis**
- 2020: *“Who was the chief minister and author of Arthashastra under Chandragupta?”* – **Chanakya**.

## Chapter: Gupta Empire (c. 320‑550  CE)

### Subtopic: Golden Age – Science, Art & Literature

**Notes**
- Known as **“Classical Age of India”** – major advances in **Mathematics**, **Astronomy**, **Medicine**.
- Prominent scholars: **Aryabhata** (π, zero), **Kalidasa** (Shakuntala), **Sushruta** (surgery).
- **Economy**: Agrarian base, trade with **Roman Empire**, **Silk Road**.
- **Religion**: **Hinduism** regained prominence; **Buddhism** declined.

**Facts**
- **Decimal system** with place‑value, **concept of zero** first codified.
- **Shaka era** (0 CE) used for dating inscriptions.

**Diagrams**
- ![Aryabhata’s astronomical model](file:///C:/Users/Trayodh%20Khandalkar/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/63330b5a-af9a-4284-8f4b-225b7f5a6c88/gsnotes/assets/diagrams/aryabhatamodel.png)

**Memory Technique**
- **“SHAK‑A”** – *Science, Hinduism, Arts, Knowledge – Aryabhata* to recall Gupta contributions.

**PYQ Analysis**
- 2021: *“Which Indian mathematician introduced the concept of zero?”* – **Aryabhata** (high‑yield).
- 2024: *“Identify the period termed ‘Golden Age of India’.”* – **Gupta Empire**.

**Common Mistakes**
- Mistaking **Harsha** (7th CE) as part of Gupta period – he ruled after Gupta decline.
- Assuming **Gupta** empire covered whole sub‑continent – mainly **North‑India**.

**Revision Material**
- **Timeline**: 320 CE (Chandragupta II) → 550 CE (end of Gupta).
- **Key achievements** in bullet form.

## Chapter: Sangam Age (c. 300  BCE‑300  CE)

### Subtopic: Tamil Literature & Kingdoms

**Notes**

- **Literary works**: **Ettuthokai** (Eight Anthologies), **Pattuppāṭṭu** (Ten Idylls).
- Kings: **Cheras**, **Cholas**, **Pandyas**.
- Society: **Patronised poets**, **Trade** with **Rome** (black pepper).

## Chapter: Buddhism & Jainism (c. 6th  BCE‑5th  CE)

### Subtopic: Origins & Spread

**Notes**
- **Buddhism** founded by **Siddhartha Gautama** (≈ 563‑483  BCE).
- **Four Noble Truths**, **Eight‑fold Path**, **Concept of Anicca** (impermanence).
- **Jainism** founded by **Mahavira** (599‑527  BCE) – **Anekantavada** (many‑sidedness).
- Spread via **Ashoka’s** missionary policy – edicts in **Greek**, **Aramaic**.

**Facts**
- Major **Stupas**: **Sanchi**, **Bodh Gaya**.
- **Jain** monastic codes – **Ahimsa** (non‑violence) stricter than Buddhist.

**Diagrams**
- ![Buddhist Wheel of Dharma](file:///C:/Users/Trayodh%20Khandalkar/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/63330b5a-af9a-4284-8f4b-225b7f5a6c88/gsnotes/assets/diagrams/buddhistwheel.png)

**Memory Technique**
- **“S‑B‑A”** – *Siddhartha – Buddha – Ashoka* (Buddhism timeline).
- **“M‑J‑A”** – *Mahavira – Jain – Ahimsa* (Jain timeline).

**PYQ Analysis**
- 2020: *“Which emperor promoted Buddhism across his empire?”* – **Ashoka**.
- 2023: *“Identify the principle of non‑violence in Jainism.”* – **Ahimsa**.

**Common Mistakes**
- Mixing **Mahavira** with **Buddha** – they are founders of **different** religions.
- Assuming **Buddhism** disappeared after **Gupta** – it persisted in **Southeast Asia**.

**Revision Material**
- **Flowchart**: Origin → Core Teachings → Spread → Major Sites.

**Practice MCQs**
1. **Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of Harappan urban planning?**
   - A) Grid‑like streets
   - B) Standardised brick sizes
   - C) Large palatial complexes with decorative arches
   - D) Great Bath for public use
   > **Answer:** C – Harappan cities lacked monumental palaces with decorative arches; architecture was utilitarian.
   > **Explanation:** The Harappan focus was on sanitation, uniformity, and public utilities; palatial architecture appears later in **Mohenjo‑Daro** but not in the described form.
2. **The primary source of evidence for the early Vedic period is:**
   - A) Ashokan edicts
   - B) Rig‑Veda
   - C) Arthashastra
   - D) Buddhist stupas
   > **Answer:** B – Rig‑Veda contains hymns composed during the early Vedic era.
   > **Explanation:** Other sources belong to later periods.
3. **Which Mahajanapada had a republican form of government?**
   - A) Magadha
   - B) Kuru
   - C) Vrijji
   - D) Avanti
   > **Answer:** C – Vrijji was a gana‑sangha (republic).
   > **Explanation:** Magadha, Kuru, Avanti were monarchies.
4. **The capital of the Mauryan Empire under Chandragupta was:**
   - A) Taxila
   - B) Pataliputra
   - C) Ujjain
   - D) Mathura
   > **Answer:** B – Pataliputra.
   > **Explanation:** Served as administrative centre throughout Mauryan reign.
5. **Who introduced the concept of zero in Indian mathematics?**
   - A) Aryabhata
   - B) Brahmagupta
   - C) Bhaskaracharya
   - D) Varahamihira
   > **Answer:** A – Aryabhata (early use of zero as a placeholder).
   > **Explanation:** Brahmagupta later formalised arithmetic rules, but concept appears first with Aryabhata.
