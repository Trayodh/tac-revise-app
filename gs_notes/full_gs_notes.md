## 📚 1. HISTORY

### 🏛️ 1.1 Ancient India

#### 📖 Prehistoric India
- **Earliest hominid fossils** – *Homo erectus* at Attirampur (~1.5 Ma).n- **Stone Age phases**: Paleolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic – tool evolution from hand‑axes to polished stone axes.n- **Key sites**: Bagor (Rajasthan), Bhimbetka rock‑shelter paintings (10‑4 ka).n- **Mnemonic**: **P‑M‑N** – *Paleolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic* – *Please Make Notes*.
- **PYQ trend (2019‑2024)**: Frequently asked about *Bhimbetka* as earliest evidence of Homo sapiens.n- **Common trap**: Confusing Mesolithic microliths with Neolithic agricultural settlements.
- **Revision bullet**: Early human presence → Tool types → Major sites.

#### 📖 Indus Valley Civilization
- **Phases**: Early (3300‑2600 BCE) & Mature (2600‑1900 BCE).n- **Urban planning**: Grid streets, baked‑brick houses, Great Bath, standardized weights (4:2:1 brick ratio).n- **Economy**: Agriculture (wheat, barley, cotton), craft (beads, metallurgy), trade via standardized weights.n- **Key sites**: Harappa, Mohenjo‑Daro, Dholavira, Rakhigarhi.n- **Diagram placeholder**: ![Indus City Layout](file:///C:/Users/Trayodh%20Khandalkar/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/63330b5a-af9a-4284-8f4b-225b7f5a6c88/gsnotes/assets/maps/induscitiesmap.png)n- **Mnemonic**: **G‑B‑C‑D** – *Great Bath, Brick ratio, City grid, Drainage*.n- **PYQ trend**: Questions on *urban planning* and *standardized weights* appear in 2020, 2022, 2023 exams.n- **Common mistake**: Treating Harappa as a single city rather than a civilization.
- **Revision**: Table of phases, sites, key features.

#### 📖 Vedic Age
- **Chronology**: Early Vedic (c.1500‑1200 BCE), Later Vedic (c.1200‑600 BCE).n- **Society**: Patriarchal tribal, pastoral economy, cattle‑herding, barley & wheat cultivation.n- **Texts**: Rig‑Veda, Sama‑Veda, Yajur‑Veda, Atharva‑Veda.n- **Political units**: Janapadas – early kingdoms like Kuru, Panchala.n- **Mnemonic**: **R‑S‑Y‑A** – *Rig, Soma, Yajur, Atharva*.n- **PYQ trend**: Pastoralism & Vedic text sequence recurring in 2021, 2023.
- **Common mistake**: Mixing Early & Later Vedic characteristics.
- **Revision**: Timeline of Vedic literature and socio‑economic shift.

#### 📖 Mahajanapadas
- **Sixteen states** – Northern (Kuru, Panchala…) & Southern (Kalinga, Ashmaka…).n- **Government types**: Monarchies & republics (Vrijji, Mahā‑Māta‑Pañca).n- **Capital highlights**: Pataliputra (Magadha), Kapilavastu (Koliya).n- **Mnemonic**: **K‑P‑M‑V‑A‑C‑S‑G‑L‑R‑N‑D‑B‑S‑J‑K** – *King Peter Might Visit Various Cities…*n- **PYQ**: Republic question (Vrijji) in 2022; Magadha capital in 2024.
- **Common mistake**: Treating Kuru as a Mahajanapada.
- **Revision**: Table – Mahajanapada, Capital, Govt., Key Ruler.

#### 📖 Mauryan Empire
- **Founder**: Chandragupta (322 BCE) – aided by Chanakya (Kautilya).n- **Administration**: Centralised bureaucracy, Arthashastra, divisions – Rajyas → Vishayas → Gram‑panchayats.n- **Capital**: Pataliputra.n- **Revenue**: Land‑tax (≈ 50 % produce).n- **Ashoka’s edicts** – spread Buddhism, inscribed in Brahmi.n- **Diagram placeholder**: ![Mauryan Admin Hierarchy](file:///C:/Users/Trayodh%20Khandalkar/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/63330b5a-af9a-4284-8f4b-225b7f5a6c88/gsnotes/assets/flowcharts/mauryanadmin.png)n- **Mnemonic**: **C‑K‑P‑A‑R** – *Chandragupta, Kautilya, Pataliputra, Arthashastra, Rajyas*.
- **PYQ**: Chanakya’s role (2020), capital (2023).
- **Common mistake**: Assuming Ashoka remained Hindu throughout.
- **Revision**: One‑page chart – Ruler, Years, Capital, Reforms.

#### 📖 Gupta Empire
- **Era**: 320‑550 CE – “Golden Age”.n- **Science**: Decimal system, zero, Aryabhata (π, planetary theory).n- **Arts**: Kalidasa (Shakuntala), sculpture, temple architecture.n- **Economy**: Agrarian base, trade with Rome, Silk Road.n- **Diagram placeholder**: ![Aryabhata’s model](file:///C:/Users/Trayodh%20Khandalkar/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/63330b5a-af9a-4284-8f4b-225b7f5a6c88/gsnotes/assets/diagrams/aryabhatamodel.png)n- **Mnemonic**: **SHAK‑A** – *Science, Hinduism, Arts, Knowledge – Aryabhata*.
- **PYQ**: Zero concept (2021), Golden Age label (2024).
- **Common mistake**: Over‑extending Gupta territorial claim.
- **Revision**: Timeline + key achievements.

#### 📖 Sangam Age
- **Region**: Tamilakam (Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Andhra, Sri Lanka).n- **Literature**: Ettuthokai, Pattuppāṭṭu.n- **Kingdoms**: Cheras, Cholas, Pandyas.n- **Trade**: Poompuhar – Roman pepper trade.n- **Map placeholder**: ![Sangam Tamilakam](file:///C:/Users/Trayodh%20Khandalkar/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/63330b5a-af9a-4284-8f4b-225b7f5a6c88/gsnotes/assets/maps/sangammap.png)n- **Mnemonic**: **C‑C‑P** – *Cheras, Cholas, Pandyas*.
- **PYQ**: Ettuthokai (2022), Poompuhar port (2023).
- **Common mistake**: Conflating Sangam with Vedic literature.
- **Revision**: Table – Kingdom, Ruler, Contribution.

#### 📖 Buddhism & Jainism
- **Buddhism** – Siddhartha Gautama (563‑483 BCE). Core: Four Noble Truths, Eight‑fold Path, Anicca.n- **Jainism** – Mahavira (599‑527 BCE). Core: Ahimsa, Anekantavada.n- **Spread**: Ashoka’s edicts (Brahmi, Greek, Aramaic).n- **Sites**: Sanchi, Bodh Gaya (Buddhism); Shravanabelagola (Jain).n- **Diagram placeholder**: ![Wheel of Dharma](file:///C:/Users/Trayodh%20Khandalkar/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/63330b5a-af9a-4284-8f4b-225b7f5a6c88/gsnotes/assets/diagrams/buddhistwheel.png)n- **Mnemonic**: **S‑B‑A** – *Siddhartha, Buddha, Ashoka*; **M‑J‑A** – *Mahavira, Jain, Ahimsa*.
- **PYQ**: Ashoka’s promotion (2020), Ahimsa (2023).
- **Common mistake**: Mixing Mahavira with Buddha.
- **Revision**: Flowchart – Origin → Teachings → Spread → Major Sites.
