### Slide 29
**Title: FORMATION OF CONTINENTS**
**CONTINENTAL DRIFT THEORY (THEORY #1)**
*   **Given by:** Alfred Wegener
*   **Key Terms:**
    *   Land- Pangea........ Laurasia & Gondwana
    *   Water- Panthalassa
*   **Visuals:** Maps showing "BEFORE" (Pangaea) and "AFTER" (current continental positions with Arctic, Atlantic, Pacific, and Indian oceans).

**PLATE TECTONIC THEORY (THEORY #2)**
*   **Proposed By:** McKenzie and Parker
*   **Core Concepts:**
    *   The earth’s lithosphere is broken into distinct plates which are floating on a ductile layer called asthenosphere (upper part of the mantle).
    *   The movement of these crustal plates (due to convection currents in the mantle) causes the formation of various landforms and is the principal cause of all movements.
*   **Map Labels (Major/Minor Plates):** Juan De Fuca Plate, North American Plate, Pacific Plate, Cocos Plate, Nazca Plate, Caribbean Plate, South American Plate, Antarctic Plate, African Plate, Arabian Plate, Indian Plate, Eurasian Plate, Philippine Plate, Australian Plate.
