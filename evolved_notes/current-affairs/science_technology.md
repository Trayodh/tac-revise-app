# Science, Technology & Space — Current Affairs for NDA/CDS 2026

## 📋 QUICK OVERVIEW

The period leading up to NDA/CDS 2026 exams has seen significant strides in India's defence capabilities and technological prowess, particularly in the domains of indigenous development and strategic systems. Key highlights include the induction of India's first indigenous MALE drone, the Drishti 10 Starliner, and advanced variants of air defence systems like Akash-IX and Project Kusha. DRDO has showcased India's ambition in high-tech warfare with successful tests of a Hypersonic Glide Vehicle (HGV) and the Rudram-2 Anti-Radiation Missile. Naval advancements include the delivery of the highly indigenous Survey Vessel 'Sanshodhak' and extensive international expeditions by INS Sudarshini, demonstrating India's growing maritime reach and collaboration. These developments underscore a strong focus on 'Atmanirbhar Bharat' (self-reliant India) in critical technology sectors.

## 📌 CRITICAL POINTS TO REMEMBER

*   **Indigenous Defence Prowess**: India's commitment to 'Make in India' in defence is evident across all services – the Navy's MALE drone (Drishti 10) and Survey Vessels (Sanshodhak), and the Army's Akash-IX missile system.
*   **DRDO's Strategic Leap**: The successful test-firing of the Hypersonic Glide Vehicle (Mach 7, 1200 km range) marks a significant advancement in India's strategic deterrent capabilities, placing it among a select few nations.
*   **Air Defence Augmentation**: The induction of Akash-IX (70 km range, dual-mode seeker) for the Army and the advanced Rudram-2 (Anti-Radiation Missile) for the Air Force, alongside the critical Project Kusha (Long-Range SAM), substantially enhances India's multi-layered air defence shield.
*   **Naval Reach and Diplomacy**: INS Sudarshini's 'Escale à Sète' and Lokayan 26 expeditions demonstrate the Indian Navy's capabilities for long-range deployments, international engagement, and soft power projection.
*   **Missile Modernization**: Continuous testing and induction of ballistic missiles like Agni-4 IRBM highlight India's focus on maintaining credible minimum deterrence.
*   **Key Manufacturers**: Adani Defence and Aerospace (Drishti 10) and GRSE (Survey Vessels) are prominent private and public sector players contributing to indigenous defence production.

## 🧠 MNEMONICS & MEMORY TRICKS

1.  **Drishti 10 Starliner**: Think "**D**rone, **D**rishti, **D**efence (Adani), **10**-star performance in the sky (Starliner)." It's the **first indigenous MALE** drone.
2.  **Hypersonic Glide Vehicle (HGV)**: "**H**yper-fast, **H**yper-range: **H**GV goes **Mach 7**, **1200** km. Remember "7-12" for speed and distance.
3.  **Akash-IX**: "**A**kash means sky/air, so it's a **S**urface-to-**A**ir **M**issile (SAM). The 'IX' can remind you of 'next-gen' or 'improved X'. 70 km is roughly '70 in the sky'.
4.  **Rudram-2**: "Think of 'Rudra' (Lord Shiva, the destroyer) and 'radiation'. So, **R**udram-2 is an **A**nti-**R**adiation **M**issile (ARM). The '2' can signify 'next-gen'."
5.  **Survey Vessels (Sandhayak Class)**: Remember the acronym **SINS**: **S**andhayak, **I**kshak, **N**irdeshak, **S**anshodhak. These ships "survey" the seas for us. 'Sanshodhak' is the **fourth** and latest delivered.
6.  **Project Kusha**: "Kusha is related to Lord Rama, often depicted with a bow and arrow, symbolising a long-range weapon. **Kusha** = **L**ong-**R**ange **S**urface-to-**A**ir **M**issile system."

## ⚠️ EXAM TRAPS & COMMON MISTAKES

*   **Interchanging Missile Types**: Confusing Akash (SAM), Rudram (Anti-Radiation Missile), Agni (Ballistic Missile) or their specific variants.
*   **Service & Induction**: Misidentifying which service (Army, Navy, Air Force) inducted a particular system (e.g., Drishti 10 for Navy, Akash-IX for Army).
*   **Indigenous vs. Imported**: Assuming a system is indigenous when it might be licensed production, or vice-versa. Almost all events here stress "indigenous".
*   **Key Numbers**: Mixing up the range (70 km for Akash-IX, 1200 km for HGV) or speed (Mach 7 for HGV).
*   **Platform Specificity**: Forgetting that Rudram-2 was flight-tested from an Su-30MKI.
*   **Names and Classes**: Confusing 'Drishti 10 Starliner' with other drones, or 'Sanshodhak' with other naval ships, or knowing it's part of the 'Survey Vessel (Large)' class.
*   **Event Context**: Forgetting why INS Sudarshini was in France (400th French Navy anniversary) or the scale of Lokayan 26.
