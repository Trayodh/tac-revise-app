# Indian Military Operations — Current Affairs for NDA/CDS 2026

## 📋 QUICK OVERVIEW

The theme "Indian Military Operations" encompasses a broad spectrum of activities ranging from direct combat and strategic deployments to humanitarian assistance, disaster relief (HADR), and non-combatant evacuation operations (NEOs). For NDA/CDS 2026, understanding these operations is crucial, as they reflect India's evolving geopolitical strategies, defence capabilities, and commitment to regional stability and global peace. Key trends observed include:

*   **Expanded HADR and NEO Footprint:** India's increasing role as a "first responder" in the Indo-Pacific and beyond, demonstrating capacity for rapid deployment during crises.
*   **Focus on Neighbourhood First and SAGAR (Security and Growth for All in the Region) Doctrines:** Operations often align with these foreign policy principles, particularly with maritime neighbours.
*   **Indigenous Defence Prowess:** A strong push for Atmanirbhar Bharat (self-reliant India) in defence, visible in new inductions, R&D, and strategic manufacturing initiatives.
*   **Strengthening International Partnerships:** Frequent joint military exercises and defence dialogues with diverse nations to enhance interoperability and strategic alignment.
*   **Modernisation of Armed Forces:** Investment in new technologies, infrastructure, and strategic bases to meet contemporary and future security challenges.
*   **Prominent Role for Women in Uniform:** Increasing opportunities and recognition for women in combat and leadership roles.
*   **Commitment to UN Peacekeeping:** Continued significant contributions to UN missions globally.

This master note provides a comprehensive overview of recent and ongoing military operations, exercises, strategic developments, and notable achievements relevant for your exam.
