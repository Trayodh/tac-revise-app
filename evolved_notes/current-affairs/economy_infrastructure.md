# Economy & Infrastructure — Current Affairs for NDA/CDS 2026

## 📋 QUICK OVERVIEW

The first half of 2026 has been dynamic for India's economic and infrastructural landscape. Key highlights include robust **GDP growth** reflecting a healthy economic trajectory, coupled with the **Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) monetary policy adjustments** to curb inflation. Significant legislative steps were taken, notably the **Defence Procurement Bill, 2026**, aimed at boosting indigenous manufacturing, and the **Environmental Protection (Amendment) Bill, 2026**, emphasizing sustainable infrastructure development. The intersection of development and conservation also came to the forefront with the Supreme Court's intervention to balance **solar infrastructure projects with Great Indian Bustard (GIB) protection**. Furthermore, advancements in digital governance were seen with the introduction of the **SMART tool by the Delhi Cantonment Board** to enhance civic services and sanitation. These events collectively paint a picture of an economy focused on growth, self-reliance, sustainability, and digital transformation.

## 📌 CRITICAL POINTS TO REMEMBER

*   **GDP & Economic Performance:** India's economy showed strong growth at **7.2%** in Q1 FY2026-27, driven by manufacturing and services. This indicates resilience and positive momentum.
*   **Monetary Policy:** The **RBI hiked the repo rate by 25 bps to 6.75%** to combat inflation, especially food-price driven, signaling a cautious stance on price stability. This was the first hike in the fiscal year 2025-26.
*   **Defence Indigenization:** The **Defence Procurement Bill, 2026**, with its **15% indigenous offset clause**, is a cornerstone of boosting domestic defence manufacturing and reducing import dependence.
*   **Sustainable Infrastructure:** The **Environmental Protection (Amendment) Bill 2026** is a significant move towards greener infrastructure by mandating **eco-friendly construction materials** in government projects and imposing stricter penalties.
*   **Development vs. Environment:** The SC's committee on the **Great Indian Bustard (GIB)** highlights the ongoing challenge of balancing critical infrastructure (solar power) with ecological conservation. Rajasthan is the key state here.
*   **Digital Governance:** The **SMART tool** by **Delhi Cantonment Board** showcases the adoption of digital solutions for improving civic services, sanitation, and data management.

## ⚠️ EXAM TRAPS & COMMON MISTAKES

*   **Mixing up GDP figures:** Be careful with the exact percentage (7.2%) and the fiscal quarter (Q1 FY2026-27). Don't confuse it with previous or projected figures.
*   **Incorrect Repo Rate:** Remember both the new rate (6.75%) and the basis points hike (25 bps). Ensure you link it to the correct body (RBI) and reason (inflation).
*   **Defence Procurement Bill specifics:** The **15% offset for indigenous production** is a key detail; don't confuse it with other percentages or provisions.
*   **Environmental Bill's Mandate:** While the bill covers stricter penalties, its unique infrastructure angle is the mandate for **eco-friendly construction materials** in *government projects*. Don't generalize this to all construction.
*   **GIB Context:** Remember the **Supreme Court committee** and the specific challenge of **solar power lines in Rajasthan** causing issues for the GIB. Don't just recall GIB generally.
*   **SMART Tool:** Attribute it correctly to the **Delhi Cantonment Board** and its purpose for **sanitation and civic service monitoring**. Avoid confusing it with other smart city initiatives or different agencies.
*   **Fiscal Year Confusion:** Pay attention to which fiscal year (FY2026-27, FY2025-26) an event is referencing, especially for economic data and RBI actions.
