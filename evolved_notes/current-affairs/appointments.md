    *   **Appointee:** Justice Sanjiv Khanna.
    *   **Successor:** Succeeded the outgoing Chief Justice.

## 🧠 MNEMONICS & MEMORY TRICKS

*   **CDS - Kargil:** Think "CDS Kares" (CDS cares) for Kargil Review Committee. Or, **C**DS from **K**argil, 'C' and 'K' are close phonetically.
*   **B.P. Singh (DGONA):** **B.P.** sounds like **B**attleship **P**ower or **B**lue**P**rint for armaments. He manages the 'BP' (Blood Pressure/power) of the Navy's armament.
*   **Ran Samwad:** "Ran" means battle. A "Ran Samwad" is a battle-dialogue or strategic discussion among the services. *Samwad* means dialogue.
*   **Justice Sanjiv Khanna (CJI):** "Sanjiv" means 'giving life' or 'reviving'. Justice Sanjiv Khanna is at the pinnacle of the judiciary, giving life to laws and justice. *Sanjiv for Justice*.

## ⚠️ EXAM TRAPS & COMMON MISTAKES

1.  **Confusing Committees:** Incorrectly attributing the CDS creation to a different committee (e.g., Naresh Chandra Task Force, Shekatkar Committee).
2.  **Misidentifying CDS Role:** Believing CDS is only an administrative head, not a principal military advisor or permanent COSC chairman.
3.  **Incorrect Names/Designations:** Mixing up B.P. Singh's post or Sanjiv Khanna's designation.
4.  **Date Confusion:** While the raw events specify 2026, be aware that the *creation* of CDS was historical (2019/2020). The January 2026 event in the prompt implies a re-highlighting or specific policy discussion in 2026. For exams, if a question asks *when* it was created, refer to the historical date; if it asks about a 2026 development, use the provided context.
5.  **Ran Samwad Details:** Forgetting it's 'tri-service' or misattributing its organizer (e.g., to Naval HQ instead of Air Force Training Command).
6.  **Succession Order:** Not knowing who Justice Sanjiv Khanna succeeded, though the prompt only mentions "outgoing Chief Justice." In a real exam, they might ask for the predecessor's name.

**1. The post of Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) in India, highlighted in January 2026, was initially created based on the recommendations of which of the following committees?**
A) Shekatkar Committee
B) Naresh Chandra Task Force
C) Kargil Review Committee
D) Godbole Committee

**Answer: C) Kargil Review Committee**

**2. In March 2026, B.P. Singh, an officer of the Indian Naval Armament Service (INAS), assumed charge as the Director General of which of the following organizations?**
A) Naval Air Arm
B) Naval Logistics
C) Naval Armament
D) Naval Intelligence

**Answer: C) Naval Armament**

**3. Justice Sanjiv Khanna took oath as the new Chief Justice of India (CJI) in August 2026. The Chief Justice of India serves as the head of which of the following?**
A) The Parliament of India
B) The Union Cabinet
C) The Supreme Court of India
D) The Election Commission of India

**Answer: C) The Supreme Court of India**

**4. The second edition of Ran Samwad, a tri-service strategic seminar, was inaugurated by Chief of Defence Staff General Anil Chauhan in April 2026. This seminar was organized by which of the following commands?**
A) Indian Army Training Command
B) Indian Navy Training Command
C) Air Force Training Command

**Answer: C) Air Force Training Command**
