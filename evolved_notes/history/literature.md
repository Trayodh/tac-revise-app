# Indian Literature

## 1. Ancient Literature

### A. Vedic Literature
*   **The [[Vedas]]:** The oldest layers of Sanskrit [[literature]].
    *   **Rig Veda:** The oldest Veda. A collection of 1028 hymns dedicated to deities like Agni, Indra, and Soma. Contains the famous *Gayatri Mantra*.
    *   **Sama Veda:** The Veda of melodies and chants; the foundational text for Indian classical music.
    *   **Yajur Veda:** Contains rituals and sacrificial formulas.
    *   **Atharva Veda:** Contains magical spells, charms, and the earliest principles of Ayurveda.
*   **The [[Upanishads]] (Vedanta):** Philosophical texts dealing with the concepts of Atman (soul), Brahman (ultimate reality), Karma, and Moksha. The phrase *Satyameva Jayate* is taken from the Mundaka Upanishad.
*   **The Epics:**
    *   **Ramayana:** Written by Sage Valmiki.
    *   **Mahabharata:** Written by Sage Vyasa. It is the longest epic poem in the world. The *Bhagavad Gita* is a part of the Bhishma Parva of the Mahabharata.

### B. Classical Sanskrit Literature
The [[Gupta Period]] is considered the Golden Age of Classical Sanskrit [[literature]].

*   **Kalidasa:** The greatest poet and dramatist of ancient India. 
    *   *Plays:* **Abhijnanashakuntalam** (The Recognition of Shakuntala), Malavikagnimitram, Vikramorvasiyam.
    *   *Epic Poems:* Raghuvamsha, Kumarasambhava.
    *   *Lyric Poetry:* **Meghaduta** (The Cloud Messenger).
*   **Shudraka:** Wrote **Mricchakatika** (The Little Clay Cart), a realistic play about the love between a poor merchant and a courtesan.
*   **Vishakhadatta:** Wrote **Mudrarakshasa** (a political thriller about Chanakya and [[Chandragupta]] Maurya) and *Devichandraguptam*.
*   **Bhasa:** An earlier playwright, famous for *Svapnavasavadattam*.
*   **Bana Bhatta:** Court poet of Harshavardhana. Wrote **Harshacharita** (biography of [[Harsha]]) and *Kadambari*.

### C. Sangam Literature (Tamil)
The earliest [[secular]] [[literature]] of South India, composed in ancient Tamil assemblies (Sangams) patronized by the Pandyas.
*   **Tolkappiyam:** Authored by Tolkappiyar. The earliest available Tamil [[work]], a treatise on grammar and poetics.
*   **The Epics:**
    *   **Silappadikaram:** (The Jeweled Anklet) by Ilango Adigal. A tragic love story of Kovalan and Kannagi.
    *   **Manimekalai:** by Sattanar. A Buddhist epic continuing the story of Kovalan's daughter.

## 2. Medieval Literature

### A. Indo-Persian Literature
Persian became the official court language during the [[Delhi Sultanate]] and Mughal periods.
*   **Amir Khusrau:** Known as the *Tuti-e-Hind* (Parrot of India). A disciple of Nizamuddin Auliya. He is credited with inventing the Sitar and the Tabla, and creating the *Khyal* and *Tarana* styles of music. He wrote the *Khaza'in ul-Futuh* and *Tughlaq Nama*.
*   **Ziauddin Barani:** A famous historian of the [[Delhi Sultanate]]. Wrote *Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi*.
*   **Abul Fazl:** The grand vizier of [[Akbar]]. Wrote the monumental **Akbarnama** (which includes the detailed administrative manual, *Ain-i-Akbari*).
*   **Dara Shikoh:** The scholarly son of [[Shah Jahan]]. He translated the [[Upanishads]] into Persian (*Sirr-i-[[Akbar]]*) and wrote *Majma-ul-Bahrain* (The Mingling of Two Oceans), arguing the fundamental unity of Hinduism and Islam.

### B. Bhakti and Vernacular Literature
[[The Bhakti movement]] popularized the use of regional vernacular languages (Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Awadhi) over classical Sanskrit, bringing [[literature]] to the common people.

*   **Tulsidas:** Wrote the **Ramcharitmanas** (the Ramayana in Awadhi), a masterpiece of Hindu devotion.
*   **Surdas:** Wrote the *Sursagar* in Braj Bhasha, describing the childhood of [[Krishna]].
*   **Mirabai:** Composed intensely emotional devotional songs (Bhajans) in Rajasthani/Hindi dedicated to [[Krishna]].
*   **Kabir:** His philosophical couplets (*Dohas*) are highly revered and preserved in the *Bijak*.

## 3. Modern Indian Literature

### A. The Bengal Renaissance and Nationalism
*   **Bankim Chandra Chatterjee:** Wrote the novel **Anandamath** (set against the backdrop of the Sannyasi Rebellion), which contains the national song **Vande Mataram**.
*   **Rabindranath Tagore:** The first Asian to win the **Nobel Prize in [[Literature]] (1913)** for his collection of poems, **Gitanjali** (Song Offerings). He composed the national anthems of both India (*Jana Gana Mana*) and Bangladesh (*Amar Sonar Bangla*).
*   **Sarat Chandra Chattopadhyay:** A highly popular Bengali novelist who wrote about the tragedy of middle-class life and the plight of women (e.g., *Devdas*, *Parineeta*).

### B. Hindi and Urdu Literature
*   **Munshi Premchand:** Considered the greatest Hindi-Urdu novelist. He moved [[literature]] away from romantic fantasies to stark social realism, highlighting the plight of the peasantry and the poor. Famous works: **Godan**, *Gaban*, *Karmabhoomi*.
*   **Mirza Ghalib:** The greatest Urdu poet of the 19th century, known for his profound and melancholy Ghazals written during the decline of the [[Mughal Empire]].
*   **Muhammad Iqbal:** A great philosopher and poet who wrote the famous patriotic song **Sare Jahan Se Achha**. However, he later became the spiritual father of the Pakistan movement.
