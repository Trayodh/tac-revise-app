# Sikh History: The Ten Gurus and the Khalsa

## 1. Introduction
Sikhism originated in the Punjab region in the 15th century, founded by Guru Nanak Dev Ji. It evolved from a peaceful spiritual movement emphasizing the unity of God and [[equality]] of all humans into a martial community (the Khalsa) in response to intense persecution by the [[Mughal Empire]].

## 2. The Ten Sikh Gurus

| Guru | Key Contributions / Events |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Guru Nanak Dev (1469-1539)** | Founder of Sikhism. Emphasized *Naam Japo* (remembering God), *Kirat Karo* (honest labor), and *Vand Chhako* (sharing). Started the institution of **Langar** (community kitchen) to break caste barriers. |
| **2. Guru Angad (1504-1552)** | Standardized the **Gurmukhi script**. Popularized and expanded the Langar system. |
| **3. Guru Amar Das (1479-1574)** | Established the *Manji* system (administrative units) to spread Sikhism. Strongly opposed the practices of Sati and Purdah. |
| **4. Guru Ram Das (1534-1581)** | Founded the city of Ramdaspur, which later became **Amritsar**. Began the excavation of the holy tank (*Amrit Sarovar*). |
| **5. Guru Arjan Dev (1563-1606)** | Compiled the **Adi Granth** (the first official edition of the Sikh scripture). Built the **Harmandir Sahib (Golden Temple)** in the middle of the sarovar. **Martyrdom:** Executed by the Mughal Emperor **[[Jahangir]]**, making him the first Sikh martyr. This event fundamentally shifted Sikhism towards militarization. |
| **6. Guru Hargobind (1595-1644)** | Transformed Sikhs into a martial community. Adopted the concept of **Miri and Piri** (temporal and spiritual authority), represented by two swords. Built the **Akal Takht** (Throne of the Timeless One) facing the Golden Temple. |
| **7. Guru Har Rai (1630-1661)** | Maintained the Sikh army but avoided direct conflict with the Mughals. Established an Ayurvedic hospital. |
| **8. Guru Har Krishan (1656-1664)** | The youngest Guru (became Guru at age 5). Died of smallpox in Delhi while serving the sick during an epidemic. |
| **9. Guru Tegh Bahadur (1621-1675)** | Founded the city of Anandpur Sahib. **Martyrdom:** Executed by the Mughal Emperor **[[Aurangzeb]]** in Chandni Chowk, Delhi, for refusing to convert to Islam and for defending the religious freedom of Kashmiri Pandits. |
| **10. Guru Gobind Singh (1666-1708)** | The last human Guru. Created the **Khalsa** (The Pure). Compiled the *Dasam Granth*. Before his death, he declared the holy scripture, the **Guru Granth Sahib**, as the eternal, living Guru of the Sikhs. |

## 3. The Creation of the Khalsa (1699)
*   **Event:** On the day of Baisakhi in 1699, Guru Gobind Singh gathered his followers at Anandpur Sahib. He asked for volunteers willing to sacrifice their heads. Five men stepped forward; they were baptized as the **Panj Pyare** (The Five Beloved Ones).
*   **Significance:** This created the **Khalsa Panth**, a community of initiated Sikhs dedicated to defending their faith and protecting the weak. 
*   **The 5 Ks:** Initiated Sikhs were mandated to wear five articles of faith:
    1.  *Kesh* (uncut hair)
    2.  *Kanga* (a wooden comb)
    3.  *Kara* (an iron bracelet)
    4.  *Kachera* (cotton underwear)
    5.  *Kirpan* (a steel sword)
*   **Names:** Men were given the surname **Singh** (Lion), and women were given the surname **Kaur** (Princess).

## 4. Post-Guru Period and The Sikh Empire

### Banda Singh Bahadur (1708 – 1716)
*   Following the death of Guru Gobind Singh, Banda Singh Bahadur took command of the Khalsa army.
*   He abolished the Zamindari system in the areas he conquered and struck coins in the names of Guru Nanak and Guru Gobind Singh.
*   He established the first independent Sikh rule in Punjab.
*   He was captured and brutally executed by the Mughal Emperor Farrukhsiyar in Delhi.

### The Misls and Maharaja Ranjit Singh (Late 18th - 19th Century)
*   After a period of severe persecution by the Mughals and Afghan invaders (Ahmad Shah Abdali), the Sikhs organized themselves into 12 [[sovereign]] military confederacies known as **Misls**.
*   **Maharaja Ranjit Singh (1780 – 1839):** 
    *   Known as the **Sher-e-Punjab** (Lion of Punjab), he belonged to the Sukerchakia Misl.
    *   He successfully united all the misls and established the powerful **Sikh Empire** with its capital at **Lahore**.
    *   He modernized his army (*Fauj-i-Khas*) by hiring European mercenaries.
    *   Covered the upper floors of the Harmandir Sahib with gold, giving it the name 'Golden Temple'.
*   **Anglo-Sikh Wars:** After Ranjit Singh's death, the empire fell into chaos. The British [[East India Company]] fought two bloody wars with the Sikhs (1845-46 and 1848-49), eventually annexing Punjab into the British Empire in 1849 under Lord Dalhousie.
