Comprehensive Guide: Literature & Poetry Across Eras and States

1. Literary Masterpieces by Historical Era

  • Ancient Era:
    • Vedic & Sanskrit: The Vedas, Upanishads. Pāṇini's Ashtadhyayi. Kalidasa's Abhijnanasakuntalam, Meghaduta. Banabhatta's Harshacharita.
    • Buddhist & Jain: Tripitakas, Jataka Tales, Milinda Panha (Pali). Agamas (Prakrit).
    • Sangam Era (Tamil): Tolkappiyam (grammar). Epics: Silappatikaram (Ilango Adigal), Manimekalai (Chithalai Chathanar).
  • Medieval Era (Persian, Vernacular & Bhakti):
    • Persian: Baburnama (Babur), Akbarnama (Abul Fazl).
    • Bhakti/Sufi Texts: Tulsidas's Ramcharitmanas (Awadhi), Surdas's Sursagar (Braj Bhasha), Kabir's Dohas. Sufi mystical verses by Amir Khusrau.
  • Modern & Nationalist Era:
    • Characterized by literature addressing social reform and Indian independence. Dinabandhu Mitra's Nil Darpan (play), Bankim Chandra's Anandamath, Rabindranath Tagore's Gitanjali.
