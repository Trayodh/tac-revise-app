Comprehensive Guide: Paintings & Traditional Arts

1. Evolution of Indian Paintings by Historical Era

  • Pre-Historic Era: Bhimbetka Rock Shelters (Madhya Pradesh). Stick figures, hunting scenes, dancing, mostly in red and white pigments.
  • Ancient Era (Murals):
    • Ajanta Caves (Maharashtra): Fresco-secco murals depicting Jataka tales, Bodhisattva Padmapani and Vajrapani. Zenith of Buddhist art.
    • Bagh Caves (MP): Secular and Buddhist murals.
    • Sittanavasal (TN): Jain murals under Pandyan rule.
  • Medieval Era (Miniatures):
    • Mughal School: Peaked under Jahangir. Secular themes, court scenes, flora/fauna (Ustad Mansur). Introduced foreshortening and 3D effects.
    • Rajput & Pahari Schools: Focus on religious themes, Krishna Leela, Ragamala, and Nayika Bheda. Notable sub-schools: Kangra, Basholi, Mewar, Kishangarh.
  • Modern Era:
    • Company School: Patna Kalam (Indian subjects for British patrons).
    • Raja Ravi Varma: Fused Indian mythological themes with Western realistic oil painting techniques.
    • Bengal School of Art: Led by Abanindranath Tagore and Nandalal Bose. Rejected European academic art in favor of pan-Asian and traditional Indian aesthetics (e.g., wash technique, Ajanta influence).

2. Traditional Folk Paintings & Arts by State

State/Region Key Traditional Arts & Paintings
Andhra Pradesh & Telangana Kalamkari (Srikalahasti and Machilipatnam), Cheriyal Scroll Painting, Nirmal Paintings
Bihar Madhubani (Mithila) Art (vibrant colors, natural dyes, mythological themes, no empty spaces), Manjusha Art, Tikuli Art
Gujarat Pithora Painting, Mata Ni Pachedi, Rogan Art (Kutch)
Himachal Pradesh Kangra & Basholi Miniatures (Pahari School)
Jharkhand Sohrai and Khovar Paintings, Jadopatia Scroll Paintings, Dhokra (Bell Metal) Art
Karnataka Mysore Painting (gesso work, delicate lines), Chittara (Deewaru community), Kasuti embroidery
Kerala Kerala Mural Paintings (temple and palace walls, high pigment density)
Madhya Pradesh Gond Art (vibrant dots and lines), Bagh Prints, Bhil Art
Maharashtra Warli Painting (geometric shapes, white pigment on red ochre mud base)
Odisha Pattachitra (cloth scroll painting, Lord Jagannath themes), Saura Art (similar to Warli)
Rajasthan Phad (scrolls), Bani Thani (Kishangarh school, 'Mona Lisa of India'), Pichhwai (Nathdwara cloth paintings of Shrinathji), Usta Art (Bikaner)
Sikkim & Himalayan belt Thangka (Buddhist scroll paintings on silk/cotton, mandalas and deities)
Tamil Nadu Tanjore (Thanjavur) Painting (3D effect, real gold foil, embedded glass beads/gems)
Uttarakhand Aipan (ritualistic folk art on red background with rice paste)
West Bengal Kalighat Painting, Patua (scrolls), Alpana
