Comprehensive Guide: Regional Festivals & Religious Traditions

1. Religious Evolution Across Eras

  • Ancient Era: Transition from Vedic ritualism (Yajnas) to the philosophical inquiries of the Upanishads. Emergence of Shramana traditions (Buddhism and Jainism) as counter-movements. Formulation of the Six Orthodox Schools (Shad-Darshanas).
  • Medieval Era: The Bhakti Movement (Alvars and Nayanars in the South, spreading North) championed personal devotion over ritual. Concurrently, the Sufi movement emphasized mystical union with the divine. This era saw the birth of Sikhism (Guru Nanak) synthesizing devotion and community service.
  • Modern Era: Socio-religious reform movements (Brahmo Samaj, Arya Samaj, Ramakrishna Mission) aimed at cleansing practices and resisting colonial cultural hegemony. Syncretism and mass public celebrations (like Tilak's Ganesh Chaturthi) became tools for nationalist mobilization.
