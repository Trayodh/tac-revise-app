## **I. Straits**

A **strait** is a naturally formed, narrow, typically navigable waterway that connects two larger bodies of water and separates two landmasses. They are of immense geopolitical and economic significance.

### **1. Definition & Significance**

*   **Definition:** A narrow passage of water connecting two seas or two other large areas of water.
*   **Significance:**
    *   **Trade Routes:** Straits are vital maritime arteries, facilitating global trade by shortening shipping distances and reducing costs.
    *   **Geopolitical Choke Points:** Their narrowness makes them strategic locations for naval control, military operations, and potential blockades, impacting international relations and security.
    *   **Cultural & Historical Exchange:** Historically, straits have been corridors for human migration, cultural diffusion, and military campaigns.
    *   **Ecological Importance:** They often exhibit unique marine ecosystems due to the mixing of different water bodies and currents.
