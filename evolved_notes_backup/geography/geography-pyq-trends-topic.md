# Geography PYQ Trends (NDA/CDS)

---

## **Geography PYQ Analysis & Recurring Themes**

Analysis of [[UPSC]] GAT Geography section indicates these recurring high-yield themes over the past decade. Mastery of these specific topics provides the highest return on investment for your preparation time.

### **1. Physical Geography & Climatology**

*   **Atmospheric Layers:** Repeatedly tested.
    *   *[[Troposphere]]:* All weather phenomena occur here.
    *   *[[Stratosphere]]:* Contains the ozone layer and is ideal for flying commercial aircraft due to the absence of weather turbulence.
    *   *Ionosphere:* Reflects radio waves back to Earth, crucial for communication.
*   **Local Winds:** Match-the-following questions are extremely common.
    *   *Chinook:* Warm and dry wind in the USA (Rockies), known as the "Snow Eater".
    *   *Fohn:* Warm and dry wind in the Alps.
    *   *Harmattan:* Dry, dusty wind in West Africa, known as the "Doctor".
    *   *Sirocco:* Hot, dry, dust-laden wind blowing from the Sahara.
*   **[[Ocean Currents]]:** Distinguishing between warm and cold currents.
    *   *Warm:* Gulf Stream, Kuroshio, Agulhas, Brazilian.
    *   *Cold:* Labrador, Canary, Benguela, Humboldt (Peru), Okhotsk.
    *   **Exam Trap:** Cold [[current]] locations on the western margins of continents are highly tested because they create deserts (e.g., Humboldt creates Atacama).

### **2. Indian Geography (Highest Question Density)**

*   **River Systems:**
    *   *East-flowing:* [[Godavari]], [[Krishna]], [[Cauvery]], Mahanadi (form deltas).
    *   *West-flowing:* [[Narmada]], [[Tapi]], Mahi, Sabarmati (form estuaries, flow through rift valleys).
    *   *Tributaries:* A favorite [[UPSC]] topic. You must know tributaries of the [[Ganga]] (Yamuna, Gomti, Ghaghara, Kosi, Son), Yamuna (Chambal, Sind, Betwa, Ken), [[Godavari]] (Penganga, Indravati), and [[Krishna]] (Tungabhadra, Bhima).
*   **[[Monsoon]] System:**
    *   Causes of the Southwest [[monsoon]] (thermal contrast, ITCZ shift, jet streams).
    *   Advance and retreat branches.
    *   **Exam Trap:** Winter rain in the Coromandel coast (Tamil Nadu) is caused by the *Northeast [[Monsoon]]*, NOT the Southwest [[monsoon]]. Winter rain in North-West India is caused by *Western Disturbances*.
*   **[[Soils]] of India:**
    *   *Alluvial:* Most widespread, highly fertile.
    *   *Black Soil (Regur):* Formed from basaltic lava, high water retention, ideal for cotton (Deccan Plateau).
    *   *Laterite Soil:* Formed by intense leaching in high rainfall areas, poor in organic matter, used for making bricks.

### **3. Mapping & Strategic Locations**

*   **Mountain Passes:**
    *   Strategic locations are frequently tested.
    *   *Zoji La:* Connects Srinagar to Kargil/Leh.
    *   *Nathu La & Jelep La:* Sikkim.
    *   *Shipki La:* Himachal Pradesh (Sutlej enters India here).
    *   *Lipulekh:* Uttarakhand (route to Kailash Mansarovar).
*   **Global Straits & Choke Points:**
    *   Strait of Malacca, Hormuz, Bab-el-Mandeb, Gibraltar. Knowing what bodies of water they connect and what landmasses they separate is mandatory.
