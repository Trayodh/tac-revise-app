## 1. Introduction and Causes
[[World War I]] (The Great War) was a global conflict primarily fought in Europe, but with major fronts in the Middle East, Africa, and parts of Asia. It fundamentally altered the geopolitical landscape of the world.

The long-term causes of the war are often remembered by the acronym **MAIN**:
1.  **Militarism:** The massive buildup of armies and navies, particularly the naval arms race between Britain and Germany.
2.  **Alliances:** Europe was divided into two rigid, heavily armed camps (The Triple Entente vs. The Triple Alliance).
3.  **Imperialism:** Fierce competition among European powers for colonies, [[resources]], and markets in Africa and Asia.
4.  **Nationalism:** Intense national pride and the desire of ethnic groups (especially in the Balkans) for independence from empires like Austria-Hungary.

**The Spark (Immediate Cause):** On June 28, 1914, **Archduke Franz Ferdinand** (heir to the Austro-Hungarian throne) was assassinated in Sarajevo by Gavrilo Princip, a Serbian nationalist. This triggered a chain reaction of alliances, plunging Europe into war within a month.

## 3. Nature of the War and Key Events
WWI was the first industrialized war, resulting in slaughter on an unprecedented scale.

*   **Trench Warfare:** On the Western Front (between Germany and France), the war bogged down into a bloody stalemate of static trench warfare stretching from the Swiss border to the North Sea. The area between trenches was known as "No Man's Land."
*   **New Technologies:** First widespread use of machine guns, poison gas (mustard gas/chlorine), tanks, airplanes, and submarines (U-boats).
*   **Key Battles:** 
    *   **Battle of the Somme (1916):** One of the bloodiest battles in human history (over 1 million casualties).
    *   **Battle of Verdun (1916):** The longest battle of the war between France and Germany.
    *   **Battle of Gallipoli (1915):** A disastrous Allied campaign (heavily involving ANZAC troops) to knock the Ottoman Empire out of the war.
*   **US Entry (1917):** The USA remained neutral until 1917. They entered the war due to Germany's policy of **unrestricted submarine warfare** (sinking the *Lusitania*) and the **Zimmermann Telegram** (a German proposal for an alliance with Mexico against the US). The influx of fresh American troops decisively tipped the balance in favor of the Allies.

## 4. The End of the War and the Treaty of Versailles
The Central Powers collapsed internally and on the battlefield in late 1918. An armistice was signed on November 11, 1918.

### The Treaty of Versailles (1919)
The peace treaty was dictated by the "Big Three" (Britain, France, and the USA) and forced upon a defeated Germany.
*   **War Guilt Clause (Article 231):** Germany was forced to accept full, sole responsibility for starting the war.
*   **Reparations:** Germany was ordered to pay crippling financial reparations to the Allies.
*   **Territorial Losses:** Germany lost 13% of its European territory (e.g., Alsace-Lorraine returned to France) and all of its overseas colonies.
*   **Military Restrictions:** The German army was limited to 100,000 men; they were forbidden from having an air [[force]] or submarines, and the Rhineland was demilitarized.

**Impact:** The treaty was seen as incredibly harsh and humiliating in Germany, creating deep resentment that directly paved the way for the rise of Adolf Hitler and [[World War II]].

## 5. Global Consequences
*   **Fall of Empires:** Four major empires collapsed: the Russian (Romanov), German (Hohenzollern), Austro-Hungarian (Habsburg), and Ottoman empires.
*   **Map of the Middle East:** The Ottoman Empire was carved up by Britain and France under the **Sykes-Picot Agreement**, creating modern borders (Iraq, Syria, Lebanon) that continue to cause conflict today.
*   **League of Nations:** Created by US [[President]] Woodrow Wilson to prevent future wars through collective security. However, it was fatally weak because it had no army and the United States refused to join it.
