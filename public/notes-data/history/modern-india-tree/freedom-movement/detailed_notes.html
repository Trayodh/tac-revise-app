Indian National Movement

The organized struggle for independence from British rule, divided into Moderate, Extremist, and Gandhian phases.

1. Early Phases

  • Indian National Congress (INC) Founded (1885): Founded by retired civil servant A.O. Hume. First session held at Bombay (W.C. Bonnerjee President, attended by 72 delegates).
  • Moderate Phase (1885-1905): Led by Naoroji, Gokhale, Banerjea. Believed in constitutional methods (Triple Ps: Prayers, Petitions, Protests).
  • Extremist Phase (1905-1919): Led by **Lal-Bal-Pal** (Lajpat Rai, Tilak, Pal) and Aurobindo Ghosh. Preached self-reliance, boycott, and passive resistance. *Partition of Bengal (1905):* Announced by Lord Curzon. Triggered the **Swadeshi and Boycott Movement**.
  • Surat Split (1907): Congress split into Moderates and Extremists over methods.
  • Lucknow Pact (1916): Moderates/Extremists reunited. INC and Muslim League signed a joint pact.
  • Home Rule League (1916): Tilak and Annie Besant started separate leagues demanding home rule.

2. Gandhian Era (1919 - 1947)

  • Gandhi returned from South Africa on 9 January 1915 (celebrated as Pravasi Bharatiya Divas).
  • Early Satyagrahas: Champaran (Bihar, 1917 - first civil disobedience against Indigo Tinkathia system), Ahmedabad Mill Strike (1918 - first hunger strike), Kheda (1918 - first non-cooperation). Mnemonic: **CAK** (Champaran, Ahmedabad, Kheda).
  • Rowlatt Act & Jallianwala Bagh (1919): Rowlatt Act allowed detention without trial. On 13 April 1919, General Dyer opened fire on a peaceful assembly at Jallianwala Bagh, Amritsar. Tagore renounced his Knighthood.
  • Non-Cooperation Movement (1920-1922): Launched alongside Khilafat movement. Called off by Gandhi in Feb 1922 after the Chauri Chaura incident (where a mob burned a police station, killing 22 policemen).
  • Quit India Movement (1942): Launched after the failure of the Cripps Mission. Gandhi gave the call **"Do or Die"** at Gowalia Tank, Bombay on 8 August 1942. Leaders were arrested overnight under *Operation Zero Hour*.
