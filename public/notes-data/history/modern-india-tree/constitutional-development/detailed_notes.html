Constitutional Development

The evolution of India's administrative and legal framework through British parliamentary interventions.

1. Company Regulation Acts

  • Regulating Act of 1773: First step by Parliament to supervise EIC. Governor of Bengal became GG of Bengal. Supreme Court at Calcutta set up.
  • Pitts India Act of 1784: Created a **Board of Control** in London to manage political affairs, establishing dual control.
  • Charter Act of 1813: Ended EIC's trade monopoly in India (except for tea and China trade). Allowed Christian missionaries.
  • Charter Act of 1833: Finished all commercial activities of EIC. GG of Bengal became **GG of India** (Bentinck).
  • Charter Act of 1853: Separated legislative and executive functions of GG's Council. Opened Civil Services to Indians.

2. Crown Administration Acts

  • Government of India Act 1858: Abolished EIC. Indian administration transferred to **British Crown**. Created Secretary of State for India.
  • Indian Councils Act 1909 (Morley-Minto Reforms): Introduced Separate Electorate for Muslims (legalized communalism). Satyendra Prasad Sinha joined Viceroy's Council.
  • Government of India Act 1919 (Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms): Introduced Dyarchy in Provinces (Transferred/Reserved subjects). Bicameral legislature at Centre.
  • Government of India Act 1935: Proposed All-India Federation (never formed). Abolished dyarchy in provinces, introduced **Provincial Autonomy**. Set up Federal Court, RBI, and divided subjects into Federal, Provincial, and Concurrent lists.
