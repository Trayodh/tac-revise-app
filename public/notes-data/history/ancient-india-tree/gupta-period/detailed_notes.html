• Gupta Period

The Gupta Empire (Golden Age: 320 - 550 CE)

An era of political unification, economic prosperity, and unmatched achievements in science, literature, and art.

1. Key Rulers & Expansion

  • Chandragupta I (319 - 335 CE): First imperial ruler. Started the Gupta Era (319 CE). Married Licchavi princess Kumaradevi.
  • Samudragupta (335 - 380 CE): Called **"Napoleon of India"** by V.A. Smith due to his military campaigns. Military conquests recorded in the **Allahabad Pillar Inscription (Prayag Prasasti)** composed by his court poet **Harishena**. Performed Ashvamedha sacrifice. Issued gold coins depicting him playing the Veena.
  • Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya: 380 - 415 CE): Defeated Sakas, annexed Western India. Court adorned by **Navaratnas** (Nine Gems, including **Kalidasa** and **Varahamihira**). Chinese pilgrim **Fa-Hien** visited India during his reign.
  • Kumargupta: Founded the famous **Nalanda University**.
  • Skandagupta: Successfully repelled the invading **Hunas** (White Huns).

2. Science, Literature & Golden Age Debate

  • Literature: Kalidasa (plays: *Abhijnanasakuntalam*, *Malavikagnimitram*; poems: *Meghaduta*, *Raghuvamsa*). Shudraka (*Mrichchakatikam*). Vishakhadatta (*Devichandraguptam*, *Mudrarakshasa*).
  • Science & Math: **Aryabhata** (wrote *Aryabhatiya*, calculated value of Pi, explained eclipses, proved Earth rotates on axis). **Varahamihira** (wrote *Brihat Samhita*, *Panchasiddhantika*). **Dhanvantari** (medicine).
  • The "Golden Age" Debate: While literature and science peaked, some historians argue that feudalism increased, foreign trade declined, and untouchability grew, questioning the "Golden" tag for the masses.
