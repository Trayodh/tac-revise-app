1. Dynasties

  • Haryanka Dynasty: Bimbisara (matrimonial alliances), Ajatashatru (patronised 1st Buddhist council).
  • Shishunaga Dynasty: Shifted capital to Vaishali, annexed Avanti.
  • Nanda Dynasty: Mahapadma Nanda (first empire builder, Ekarat), Dhana Nanda. Alexander the Great invaded India (326 BCE) during Dhana Nanda's reign, fought Battle of Hydaspes (Jhelum) with Porus.
