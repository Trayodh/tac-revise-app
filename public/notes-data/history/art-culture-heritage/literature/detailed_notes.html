Literary Traditions of India

The vast text corpus of India, preserving scientific, political, and philosophical achievements.

1. Ancient Scripts & Texts

  • The Vedas: Rigveda (1028 hymns), Samaveda (music), Yajurveda (prose/ritual instructions), Atharvaveda (medicine/spells).
  • Epics: Ramayana (Valmiki) and Mahabharata (Vyasa).
  • Buddhist Canons (Tripitakas - in Pali): Sutta Pitaka (Buddha's discourses), Vinaya Pitaka (rules for monks), Abhidhamma Pitaka (philosophical analysis).
  • Jain Canons: Angas (written in Prakrit/Ardhamagadhi).
  • Sangam Literature (Tamil): Tolkappiyam (grammar), epics Silappatikaram and Manimekalai, and Tirukkural (moral guide by Tiruvalluvar).
