• History PYQ Trends (NDA/CDS)

1. Ancient History High-Yield Patterns

  • Prehistoric India: Recurring questions on the earliest human shelters. **Bhimbetka** (MP) is repeatedly asked as the earliest evidence of Homo sapiens.
  • Indus Valley: Highlights on **Dholavira** water management (2020), significance of **Standardized Weights** (2022), and theories of decline (2023).
  • Buddhism & Jainism: Direct questions on Buddhist Councils, their patrons (Ajatashatru, Kalasoka, Ashoka, Kanishka), and the concept of Ahimsa.

2. Medieval & Modern Trends

  • Delhi Sultanate: Administrative reforms of **Alauddin Khilji** (market control) and **Firoz Shah Tughlaq** (public welfare departments).
  • British Expansion & Acts: Diwani rights of Bengal (Battle of Buxar 1764, Treaty of Allahabad 1765). Comparison of Provincial Dyarchy (1919 Act) and Provincial Autonomy (1935 Act).
