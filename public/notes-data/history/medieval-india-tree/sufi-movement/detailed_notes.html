The Sufi Movement

The mystical movement within Islam, emphasizing direct union with God through love, music (Sama), and simple living.

1. Key Orders (Silsilas) in India

  • Suhrawardi Order: Founded by Shihabuddin Suhrawardi. Active in Punjab/Sindh. Accepted state offices, wealthy order.
  • Naqshbandi Order: Founded by Khwaja Bahauddin Naqshbandi. Most orthodox and conservative order. Opposed music and liberal policies of Akbar. Supported by Aurangzeb.
