Sikh History & Gurus

Sikhism evolved from a peaceful Bhakti movement to a highly militarized community in response to Mughal persecutions.

1. The Ten Gurus

  • Guru Nanak Dev (1st): Founded Sikhism. Started **Langar** (free community kitchen) to break caste barriers.
  • Guru Angad (2nd): Standardized the **Gurmukhi script**.
  • Guru Ram Das (4th): Founded Amritsar on land gifted by Akbar.
  • Guru Arjan Dev (5th): Compiled the **Adi Granth**. Built the Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib). Executed by **Jahangir**.
  • Guru Hargobind (6th): Built the **Akal Takht**. Militarized Sikhs, introduced *Miri* (temporal power) and *Piri* (spiritual power).
  • Guru Tegh Bahadur (9th): Executed by **Aurangzeb** at Delhi's Chandni Chowk for refusing to convert.
  • Guru Gobind Singh (10th): Founded the **Khalsa Panth** in 1699 at Anandpur Sahib. Introduced the 5 Ks. Declared **Guru Granth Sahib** as the permanent Guru.

2. Maharaja Ranjit Singh & British Wars

  • Consolidated Punjab into a powerful empire. Signed the Treaty of Amritsar (1809) with Metcalfe/Minto, fixing Sutlej river as boundary.
  • Anglo-Sikh Wars: 1st War (1845-46): Ended with the Treaty of Lahore (Henry Hardinge GG).
  • 2nd War (1848-49): Under **Lord Dalhousie**. Punjab was annexed to the British Empire (1849).
