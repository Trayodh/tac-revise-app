Bahmani Kingdom & Deccan Sultanates

The major Islamic state of Deccan, which continuously warred with Vijayanagara over the fertile Raichur Doab.

1. Bahmani Kingdom (1347 - 1527)

  • Founded in 1347 by Ala-ud-Din Bahman Shah (also known as Hasan Gangu) in revolt against Muhammad bin Tughlaq.
  • Capital was initially Gulbarga, later shifted to Bidar. Mahmud Gawan (great prime minister) expanded power via administrative reforms.

2. Split into Deccan Sultanates

In the early 16th century, the Bahmani kingdom disintegrated into five independent sultanates:

  • Adil Shahis of Bijapur: Founded by Yusuf Adil Shah. Famous for building the **Gol Gumbaz** (largest dome in India, featuring a whispering gallery).
  • Nizam Shahis of Ahmadnagar: Malik Ahmad. Defended by Chand Bibi against Akbar's forces.
  • Qutb Shahis of Golconda: Quli Qutb Shah. Famous for Golconda Diamond mines. Built **Charminar** in Hyderabad.
  • Imad Shahis of Berar: Fathullah Imad-ul-Mulk.
  • Barid Shahis of Bidar: Qasim Barid.
