The Stone Age

Prehistoric India is divided into Paleolithic, Mesolithic, and Neolithic ages based on stone tool technology and lifestyle shifts.

1. Paleolithic (Old Stone Age): Up to 10,000 BCE

  • Lifestyle: Nomadic hunter-gatherers. Lived in caves and rock shelters. Fire was discovered but not widely controlled for cooking.
  • Tools: Large, unpolished stone tools made of **quartzite** (hence Paleolithic humans are called "Quartzite Men"). Hand-axes, cleavers, choppers.
  • Phases: Lower Paleolithic: Hand-axes, cleavers. Sites: Soan Valley (Punjab, Pakistan), Bori (Maharashtra).
  • Middle Paleolithic: Flake tools. Sites: Narmada Valley, Nevasa (Maharashtra).
  • Upper Paleolithic: Bone tools, blades. Coincided with modern humans (Homo sapiens). Sites: Kurnool caves (AP - evidence of ash).

2. Mesolithic (Middle Stone Age): 10,000 BCE - 6,000 BCE

  • Climatic Shift: Transition to a warmer, drier climate, leading to changes in flora and fauna.
  • Tools: **Microliths** - extremely small, pointed stone tools (1-5 cm long) fitted onto wooden or bone handles (spears, arrows).
  • Lifestyle: Hunting-gathering transitioned to early animal domestication. Dog was the first animal domesticated.
  • Key Sites: **Bagor** (Rajasthan - earliest evidence of domestication), **Adamgarh** (MP), Langhnaj (Gujarat).

3. Neolithic (New Stone Age): 6,000 BCE - 1,000 BCE

  • The Neolithic Revolution: Transition from food-gathering to **food-producing** (agriculture).
  • Lifestyle: Settled village life. Invention of the wheel, cultivation of wheat, barley, ragi, and rice. Domestication of sheep, goats, cattle. Production of hand-made and wheel-made pottery.
  • Key Sites: **Mehrgarh (Balochistan, Pakistan):** Earliest Neolithic site (~7000 BCE). Evidence of wheat/barley farming and mud-brick houses.
  • Burzahom (Kashmir): Unique pit-dwellings (caves dug in ground to escape cold) and practice of burying dogs with masters.
  • Koldihwa (UP): Earliest evidence of rice cultivation globally (~6000 BCE).
  • Chirand (Bihar): Large number of bone tools made of deer antlers.
