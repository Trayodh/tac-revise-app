COMPREHENSION Around 15 to 20 questions based on comprehension are asked in CDS exam. The questions are based on the given passages. For tips on how to attempt these questions, read the section below. Comprehension involves a thorough understanding of the given passage consisting of one or more paragraphs. It forms a very good exercise for intelligent reading and judicious selection. It is meant to test the intelligence of candidates and their ability to comprehend a given piece of text. Tips for Attempting Comprehension 1. Skim once as rapidly as possible to determine the main idea before you look at the questions. Do not worry about words you do not know at this juncture.

2. Underline the Words that you do not understand to facilitate a complete understanding of the passage. This will help you to locate the words later.

3. Look Through the Questions Carefully You are advised to maintain the order in which the questions appear in the test paper. Read intensively the portion relevant to the answer.

4. Concentrate on the vocabulary items and re-read the portions of the passage containing the words you do not understand to find out their meanings.

Usually, options are quite tricky or some options are closely related. Therefore, analyse the questions and answer options carefully before answering.
