SYNONYMS Generally, 8-12 questions are asked on synonyms in the CDS exam. The questions can be in a direct format or sentence format. A candidate is expected to have a good vocabulary to solve these questions correctly. Synonyms are the words which have a similar meaning to that of a given word. e.g. The synonyms of the word reside is ‘dwell’.

Questions on synonyms can be asked directly or in a sentence format in the CDS exam.

1. Direct Format In this format a word is given and out of the given four options, a word is to be selected which is the nearest in meaning to the given word.

e.g. Which of the given words is closest in meaning to the word provided? REMOTE (a) Automatic (b) Distant (c) Mean (d) Savage Explanation (b) Remote means located far away and ‘Distant’ also means ‘far away in space or time’. While, Automatic means done or occurring spontaneously; Mean means intend to convey or refer to and Savage means cruel and vicious. So, the right synonym for ‘Remote’ is ‘Distant’. Hence, option (b) is the correct choice.

2. Sentence Format In this format one word of a sentence is underlined or capitalized and out of the given four options, a word is to be selected which is similar in meaning to the underlined or capitalized word.

e.g. Which of the given words has the same meaning to the word written in capital letters? Getting a WEAK mobile signal in remote areas is quite common. (a) Fluctuating (b) Falling (c) Low (d) Distant Explanation (c) In this sentence ‘Weak’ means ‘Low’ mobile signal. While, Fluctuating (rise and fall); Falling (drop down) and Distant (far away) have different meanings. Hence, option (c) is the correct choice.
