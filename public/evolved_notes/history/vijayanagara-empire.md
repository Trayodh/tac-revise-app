# The Vijayanagara Empire (1336 CE – 1646 CE)

## 1. Introduction and Foundation
The [[Vijayanagara Empire]] (City of Victory) was founded in 1336 CE in the wake of the rebellions against Muhammad bin Tughlaq's rule in South India. It served as a powerful Hindu bulwark against the Islamic sultanates of the Deccan. 

*   **Founders:** **Harihara I and Bukka I** (brothers from the Sangama dynasty), originally feudatories of the Kakatiyas of Warangal, later serving the Kampili state.
*   **Spiritual Guide:** They were inspired and guided by the sage **Vidyaranya** (from the Sringeri Matha) to establish the empire.
*   **Capital:** **Hampi** (on the banks of the Tungabhadra river in Karnataka). It became one of the wealthiest and largest cities in the medieval world.
*   **Conflict:** The empire was engaged in a continuous, multi-century conflict with the neighboring **Bahmani Sultanate** (and its successor states) over the fertile Raichur Doab (the region between the [[Krishna]] and Tungabhadra [[rivers]]), the [[Krishna]]-[[Godavari]] delta, and the Marathwada region.

## 2. The Four Dynasties
The empire was ruled by four distinct dynasties:
1.  **Sangama Dynasty (1336 – 1485):**
    *   **Harihara II:** Expanded the empire across southern India.
    *   **Deva Raya I:** Built a dam across the Tungabhadra river for [[irrigation]]. The Italian traveler **Nicolo Conti** visited during his reign.
    *   **Deva Raya II (Praudha Deva Raya):** The greatest Sangama ruler. He inducted Muslim cavalrymen and archers into his army to counter the Bahmani forces. The Persian envoy **Abdur Razzaq** visited his court.
2.  **Saluva Dynasty (1485 – 1505):** Founded by Saluva Narasimha. A brief period of consolidation.
3.  **Tuluva Dynasty (1505 – 1570):** The most glorious dynasty, founded by Vira Narasimha.
    *   **[[Krishna]] Deva Raya (1509 – 1529):** (See details below).
4.  **Aravidu Dynasty (1570 – 1646):** Founded by Tirumala after the disastrous Battle of Talikota. The empire survived but as a shadow of its former self, eventually disintegrating into independent Nayaka states (Madurai, Tanjore, Keladi).

## 3. Krishna Deva Raya (1509 – 1529 CE)
The greatest emperor of Vijayanagara and one of the most illustrious rulers in Indian history.

*   **Military Conquests:** He decisively defeated the Adil Shahis of Bijapur, the Gajapatis of Odisha, and the Bahmani Sultan. He restored the Bahmani Sultan to his throne (to keep the Deccan sultanates divided) and assumed the title *Yavana-rajya-sthapana-acharya*.
*   **[[Literature]] (The Augustan Age of Telugu [[Literature]]):**
    *   [[Krishna]] Deva Raya was a great scholar himself. He wrote the famous Telugu [[work]] on statecraft, **Amuktamalyada**, and a Sanskrit drama, *Jambavati Kalyanam*.
    *   His court was adorned by the **Ashtadiggajas** (Eight Great Poets of Telugu). The most prominent among them were **Allasani Peddana** (known as *Andhra Kavita Pitamaha*) and **Tenali Ramakrishna** (author of *Panduranga Mahatmyam*).
*   **[[Architecture]]:** He built the magnificent **Vijaya Mahal**, the **Hazara Rama Temple**, and the **Vithalaswami Temple** at Hampi. He also founded a new city called Nagalapuram in memory of his mother.
*   **Foreign Relations:** He maintained friendly relations with the **Portuguese** ([[Governor]] Albuquerque), granting them permission to build a fort at Bhatkal, in exchange for a monopoly on the supply of Arabian horses. The Portuguese travelers **Domingo Paes** and **Fernao Nuniz** visited his court and left glowing accounts of the empire's wealth.

## 4. Administration and Society
*   **Nayankara (Amaram) System:** This was the most distinctive feature of Vijayanagara administration. Similar to the Iqta system of the [[Delhi Sultanate]], top military commanders (called *Nayakas*) were granted territories (*Amaram*) with a fixed revenue. In return, they maintained a specified number of troops, horses, and elephants for the emperor and paid a fixed annual tribute.
*   **Ayagar System:** At the village level, local administration was handled by a body of 12 hereditary village officials known as *Ayagars*.
*   **Economy:** Highly prosperous due to flourishing maritime trade. The empire issued a large number of gold coins called **Varahas** (or *Pagodas*).
*   **Society:** Women held a relatively high position; Nuniz mentions women working as wrestlers, astrologers, and accountants. However, social evils like *Sati* and the Devadasi system were prevalent. 

## 5. The Battle of Talikota (1565 CE)
Also known as the Battle of Rakkasa-Tangadi.
*   During the reign of Sadasiva Raya (a puppet king), the real [[power]] was held by his ambitious minister, **Aliya Rama Raya**.
*   Rama Raya actively interfered in the internal politics of the five Deccan Sultanates, trying to play them against each other.
*   Realizing the threat, the Sultanates of **Bijapur, Ahmadnagar, Golconda, and Bidar** (Berar did not join) formed a grand alliance.
*   They decisively defeated the Vijayanagara army at Talikota. Rama Raya was captured and beheaded. The magnificent city of Hampi was systematically looted, burned, and destroyed for six months, leading to the rapid decline of the empire.
