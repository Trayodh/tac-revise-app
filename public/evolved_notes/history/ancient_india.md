# Ancient India

## Harappan Civilization
- Discovered in 1921 by Daya Ram Sahni.
- Major sites: Harappa, Mohenjodaro, Lothal, Dholavira.
- Known for urban planning and drainage systems.

## Vedic Period
- Early Vedic (1500-1000 BC) and Later Vedic (1000-600 BC).
- Key texts: Rig Veda, Sama Veda, Yajur Veda, Atharva Veda.
