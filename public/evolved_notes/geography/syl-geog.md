### 1. Introduction to Indian Geography

India, a land of immense geographical diversity, presents a rich tapestry of physical features, climatic zones, and natural [[resources]]. Understanding its geography is crucial for Indian Defence Examinations like NDA, CDS, AFCAT, CAPF, INET, Agniveer, and Territorial Army, as 12-15 questions are frequently asked from this section. This module delves into the intricate details of India's [[rivers]], mountain passes, and soil types, integrating information from authoritative sources and optimizing it for comprehensive learning and retention.

### 2. Indian Geography: An Overview

India is the 7th largest country in the world by area (approximately 3.287 million sq km) and the 2nd most populous, accounting for 2.4% of the world's land area and 17.4% of its [[population]].

*   **Location & Extent:**
    *   **Latitudinal Extent:** 8°4′ N to 37°6′ N
    *   **Longitudinal Extent:** 68°7′ E to 97°25′ E
    *   **Total Land Boundary:** ~15,200 km
    *   **Total Coastline:** ~7,516.6 km (including [[islands]])
    *   **Tropic of Cancer (23.5°N):** Passes through 8 Indian states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, and Mizoram.
*   **Extreme Points of India:**
    *   **Northernmost Point:** Indira Col (Siachen Glacier, Ladakh)
    *   **Southernmost Point (Mainland):** Kanyakumari (Tamil Nadu)
    *   **Southernmost Point (Overall):** Indira Point (Pygmalion Point) (Great Nicobar Island, Andaman & Nicobar [[Islands]])
    *   **Easternmost Point:** Kibithu (Arunachal Pradesh)
    *   **Westernmost Point:** Guhar Moti (Kutch, Gujarat)
*   **Indian Standard Time (IST):**
    *   **Meridian:** 82.5° E longitude, passing through Naini (near Allahabad, Uttar Pradesh).
    *   **Time Difference:** 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of Greenwich Mean Time (GMT).
    *   **States through IST Meridian:** Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh.

### 3. Physiographic Divisions of India

India is broadly divided into five major physiographic units:
1.  The Himalayan Mountain System
2.  The Great Northern Plains
3.  The [[Peninsular Plateau]]
4.  The [[Coastal Plains]]
5.  The Indian [[Islands]]

#### 3.1. The Himalayan Mountain System

The [[Himalayas]] are geologically young and structurally fold mountains, stretching in an arc shape for about 2400 km from the [[Indus]] Gorge in the west to the [[Brahmaputra]] Gorge in the east. Their width varies from 400 km in Kashmir to 150 km in Arunachal Pradesh.

**General Characteristics:**
*   **Origin:** Formed due to the collision of the Indian Plate with the Eurasian Plate.
*   **Nature:** Young fold mountains, characterized by high peaks, deep valleys, and glacial features.
*   **Altitudinal Variation:** Greater in the eastern half than in the western half.

**Longitudinal Divisions:**

1.  **Trans-[[Himalayas]] (Tibetan [[Himalayas]]):**
    *   **Location:** North of the Greater [[Himalayas]].
    *   **Age:** Older than the main Himalayan ranges.
    *   **Features:** Act as a watershed between [[rivers]] flowing north and south.
    *   **Ranges:** Karakoram, Ladakh, Zanskar.
    *   **Average Width:** ~40 km.
    *   **Average Height:** ~5000 m.

2.  **Greater [[Himalayas]] (Himadri):**
    *   **Location:** Northernmost and most continuous range.
    *   **Average Height:** ~6000 m.
    *   **Peaks:** Contains the world's highest peaks, including Mount Everest (Nepal/China), Kanchenjunga (India), Makalu, Dhaulagiri, Nanga Parbat, Annapurna, Nanda Devi.
    *   **Inhabitants:** Mostly inhabited by Bhotias (UK, Sikkim, WB).

3.  **Lesser [[Himalayas]] (Himachal/Middle [[Himalayas]]):**
    *   **Location:** South of the Greater [[Himalayas]].
    *   **Features:** Most rugged mountain system.
    *   **Ranges:** Pir Panjal, Dhauladhar, Mussoorie, Nag Tibba.
    *   **Hill Stations:** Shimla, Mussoorie, Dharamshala, Kaosani.
    *   **Valleys:** Kashmir Valley, Kangra Valley, Kullu Valley.

4.  **Shivaliks (Outer [[Himalayas]]):**
    *   **Location:** Outermost range of the [[Himalayas]].
    *   **Width:** 10-50 km.
    *   **Altitude:** 900-1100 m.
    *   **Composition:** Composed of unconsolidated sediments brought down by [[rivers]].
    *   **Duns/Doons:** Longitudinal valleys between the Lesser [[Himalayas]] and the Shivaliks (e.g., Dehradun, Kotlidun, Patlidun).

5.  **Purvanchal Hills (Eastern Hills):**
    *   **Location:** After the Dihang Gorge, the [[Himalayas]] bend sharply southward and extend along the eastern boundary of India.
    *   **Features:** Composed of sandstone and sedimentary [[rocks]].
    *   **Ranges:** Patkai Bum, Naga Hills, Manipur Hills, Mizo Hills (Lushai Hills), Garo, Khasi, Jaintia Hills.
    *   **Highest Peaks:** Kangto (Arunachal Pradesh), Saramati (Naga Hills), Mt. Tempu (Manipur), Phawngpui (Blue Mountain, Mizoram), Shillong Peak (Meghalaya).

**Major Passes of India:**

    *   **[[Indus]] River System:**
        *   **Origin:** Bokhar Chu glacier, near Mansarovar Lake, Tibet.
        *   **Tributaries:** Zanskar, Dras, Gartang, Shyok, Shingar, Nubra, Gilgit (in J&K); Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej (join [[Indus]] in Pakistan).
        *   **Old Names of Tributaries:**
            *   Jhelum: Vitasta
            *   Chenab: Asikini
            *   Ravi: Parushni
            *   Beas: Vipasa
            *   Sutlej: Shatudri
        *   **Basin States (India):** Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Punjab, Himachal Pradesh, Rajasthan, Haryana, Chandigarh.
        *   **Special Features:** Forms a trijunction with Afghanistan and China.
        *   **Dams:** Ranjit Sagar Dam (Ravi), Bhakra Nangal Dam (Sutlej).
    *   **[[Ganga]] River System:**
        *   **Origin:** Gangotri Glacier (Gaumukh, Uttarakhand) as Bhagirathi.
        *   **Confluences (Prayags):**
            *   **Vishnuprayag:** Alaknanda + Dhauliganga
            *   **Nandprayag:** Alaknanda + Nandakini
            *   **Karnaprayag:** Alaknanda + Pindar
            *   **Rudraprayag:** Alaknanda + Mandakini
            *   **Devprayag:** Alaknanda + Bhagirathi (forms [[Ganga]])
        *   **Left Bank Tributaries:** Ramganga, Gomti, Kali/Sharda, Gandak, Kosi, Mahanadi.
        *   **Right Bank Tributaries:** Yamuna, Son.
        *   **Special Features:** Kosi is known as the 'Sorrow of Bihar', Damodar as 'Sorrow of Bengal'. Hooghly is a distributary.
        *   **Cities on [[Ganga]]:** Rishikesh, Haridwar, Kanpur, Prayagraj, Varanasi, Patna, Bhagalpur, Kolkata.
    *   **[[Brahmaputra]] River System:**
        *   **Origin:** Angsi Glacier (Tibet) as Tsangpo.
        *   **Names:** Tsangpo (Tibet), Dihang/Siang (Arunachal Pradesh), [[Brahmaputra]] (Assam), Jamuna (Bangladesh).
        *   **Tributaries:** Dibang, Lohit, Kameng, Subansiri, Manas, Teesta, Sankosh.
        *   **Special Features:** Forms Majuli, the largest riverine island in the world.

#### 3.2.2. Peninsular River Systems

These [[rivers]] are older than the Himalayan [[rivers]], mostly seasonal, and flow through stable plateaus.

*   **East Flowing [[Rivers]] (Drain into Bay of Bengal):**
    *   **[[Godavari]]:**
        *   **Origin:** Trimbakeshwar, Nashik (Maharashtra).
        *   **Tributaries:** Indravati, Manjira, Penganga, Pranahita, Wardha, Purna.
        *   **Basin States:** Maharashtra, MP, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, AP, Telangana.
        *   **Special Features:** Largest peninsular river, also called 'Dakshin [[Ganga]]'.
        *   **Dams:** Jayakwadi Dam.
    *   **[[Krishna]]:**
        *   **Origin:** Mahabaleshwar (Maharashtra).
        *   **Tributaries:** Tungabhadra, Ghataprabha, Malaprabha, Bhima, Koyna, Vedavati.
        *   **Basin States:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, AP, Telangana.
        *   **Dams:** Nagarjuna Sagar Dam, Alamatti Dam, Krishnaraja Sagar Dam.
    *   **Mahanadi:**
        *   **Origin:** Chhattisgarh.
        *   **Tributaries:** Hasdeo, Ib, Jonk, Mand, Seonath.
        *   **Basin States:** Chhattisgarh, Odisha.
        *   **Dams:** Hirakud Dam.
    *   **[[Cauvery]]:**
        *   **Origin:** Brahmagiri Hills (Karnataka).
        *   **Tributaries:** Kabini, Hemavathi, Akravati, Bhavani, Simsha.
        *   **Basin States:** Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Puducherry.
        *   **Dams:** Mettur Dam, Krishnaraja Sagar Dam.
*   **West Flowing [[Rivers]] (Drain into Arabian Sea):**
    *   **[[Narmada]]:**
        *   **Origin:** Amarkantak Plateau (MP).
        *   **Tributaries:** Amravati, Tawa, Bhukhi.
        *   **Basin States:** MP, Gujarat, Maharashtra.
        *   **Special Features:** Flows through a rift valley, forms Dhuandhar Waterfall.
        *   **Dams:** Sardar Sarovar Dam, Indira Sagar Dam.
    *   **[[Tapi]]:**
        *   **Origin:** Satpura, Betul (MP).
        *   **Basin States:** MP, Gujarat, Maharashtra.
        *   **Dams:** Ukai Dam.
    *   **Luni:**
        *   **Origin:** Near Pushkar, Aravalli Ranges (Rajasthan).
        *   **Tributaries:** Khari, Sukri, Jawai.
        *   **Special Features:** Inland drainage river, lost in Rann of Kutch.
    *   **Sabarmati:**
        *   **Origin:** Udaipur, Aravalli (Rajasthan).
        *   **Dams:** Dharoi Dam.
