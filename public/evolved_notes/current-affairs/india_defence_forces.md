# Indian Defence Forces & Modernisation — Current Affairs for NDA/CDS 2026

## 📋 QUICK OVERVIEW

The year 2026 marks significant strides in India's journey towards defence indigenization and the modernization of its air force fleet. With a strong focus on "Atmanirbhar Bharat" (self-reliant India), the Ministry of Defence and Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) have been at the forefront of key developments. These include a major procurement deal for advanced indigenous fighter jets to bolster the Indian Air Force (IAF) and the successful maiden flight of a next-generation combat aircraft prototype, aiming to replace a long-serving legacy fleet. These events underscore India's commitment to enhancing its defence capabilities through domestic research, development, and manufacturing.

## 📌 CRITICAL POINTS TO REMEMBER

1.  **Atmanirbhar Bharat in Defence**: Both events strongly emphasize India's push for self-reliance in defence manufacturing and technology development.
2.  **HAL's Central Role**: Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) is the primary manufacturer and developer for both the LCA Tejas Mk-1A and the ALCA, showcasing its critical contribution to India's aerospace industry.
3.  **IAF Modernization Strategy**:
    *   The **LCA Tejas Mk-1A** is an improved variant of the Tejas Mk-1, designed to augment and eventually replace older squadrons, enhancing the IAF's current combat capabilities.
    *   The **ALCA** represents the next leap, specifically targeted at replacing the vintage **MiG-21** fleet, which has been the backbone of the IAF for decades but is now facing obsolescence and safety concerns.
4.  **Key Distinctions**:
    *   **LCA Tejas Mk-1A**: Current production, improved version, already operational/entering service.
    *   **ALCA**: Prototype stage, future generation, specifically designed to replace MiG-21s. It is likely a more advanced platform than the current Tejas variants.
5.  **Strategic Impact**: These procurements and developments ensure a steady supply of modern fighter jets, reduce dependence on foreign suppliers, and position India as a significant player in aerospace technology.

## ⚠️ EXAM TRAPS & COMMON MISTAKES

1.  **Confusing Tejas Mk-1A and ALCA**: While both are indigenous light combat aircraft from HAL, Tejas Mk-1A is an existing platform with upgraded capabilities being inducted, whereas ALCA is a *new prototype* specifically designed to replace MiG-21s.
2.  **Incorrect Replacement Aircraft**: A common trap is to ask which aircraft the ALCA is meant to replace, with options like MiG-29, Su-30MKI, or Jaguar. The correct answer is **MiG-21**.
3.  **Misremembering Quantity**: Ensure you remember '97' for the Tejas Mk-1A order, not any other number.
4.  **Incorrect Manufacturer**: Always associate these indigenous aircraft with **HAL**.
5.  **Getting the Maiden Flight Date Wrong**: While the exact date (20 May 2026) might be tested, remember at least the month and year (May 2026).

**Q3:** Consider the following statements regarding India's indigenous defence development:
1. The Advanced Light Combat Aircraft (ALCA) prototype successfully completed its maiden flight on 20 May 2026 from Hindustan Aeronautics Limited, Bengaluru.
2. The ALCA is intended to replace the existing fleet of Dassault Rafale jets in the Indian Air Force.

a) 1 only
b) 1 and 2 only
c) 2 and 3 only

**Q1: c) MiG-21**

**Q4: c) Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan**
