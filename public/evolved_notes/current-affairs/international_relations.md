# International Relations & Diplomacy — Current Affairs for NDA/CDS 2026

## 📋 QUICK OVERVIEW

In 2026, India's foreign policy has been marked by dynamic engagement across various global and bilateral platforms, solidifying its position as a responsible and influential global actor. Key trends include:

*   **Strengthening Strategic Partnerships:** Deepening defence, technology, and economic ties with major powers like the USA (DTTI, F414 jet engines), France (MCM Suite, new PM), Australia (Defence Dialogue), and Japan (LUPEX).
*   **Proactive Economic Diplomacy:** A landmark Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement (TEPA) with the EFTA bloc, demonstrating India's commitment to diversifying trade relations and attracting investments.
*   **Enhanced Neighbourhood and Maritime Security:** Active naval diplomacy and defence cooperation with Indian Ocean Littoral States (Sri Lanka, Seychelles, Mauritius) under initiatives like SAGAR, alongside vigilant border management with China.
*   **Multilateralism and Global Responsibility:** Continued participation in UN initiatives (Peacekeepers, UNSC elections) and alignment with international environmental goals (Kunming-Montreal GBF). India also played a role in global peace efforts, such as the Ukraine Peace Summit.
*   **Soft Power and Scientific Diplomacy:** Leveraging cultural heritage (UNESCO recognition for Sarnath) and advanced space collaboration (LUPEX) to enhance global standing.
*   **Growing Defence Export Capabilities:** India is emerging as a significant defence exporter, particularly to Southeast Asian nations, reflecting its 'Make in India' and 'Aatmanirbhar Bharat' initiatives.

This period underscores India's balanced approach, combining strategic autonomy with robust international cooperation to advance its national interests and contribute to global stability.

## 📌 CRITICAL POINTS TO REMEMBER

*   **Multilateralism in Action:** India's active role in UN affairs (Peacekeepers Day, UNSC elections) and environmental treaties (Kunming-Montreal GBF) signifies its commitment to global governance and addressing shared challenges.
*   **Deepening Bilateral Ties:** Focus on strategic partnerships through defence (US, France, Australia), economic (EFTA), and scientific collaborations (Japan). These demonstrate India's multi-aligned foreign policy.
*   **India's Neighbourhood Focus:** Continued emphasis on the 'Neighbourhood First' policy through bilateral defence exercises (Sri Lanka) and naval diplomacy in the Indian Ocean (Seychelles, Mauritius).
*   **"Make in India" & Defence Exports:** The co-production of F414 engines, the L&T-Exail partnership for MCM suites, and the significant increase in defence equipment exports highlight India's drive for self-reliance and its emergence as a defence manufacturing hub.
*   **Geopolitical Awareness:** The Galwan patrol indicates the ongoing sensitivity and strategic importance of the India-China border, requiring constant vigilance and diplomatic engagement.
*   **Soft Power Projections:** The UNESCO recognition of Sarnath and joint space ventures (LUPEX) enhance India's global image through cultural heritage and scientific prowess.
*   **Economic Liberalization:** The EFTA TEPA is a significant step towards expanding India's global trade footprint, attracting foreign investment and boosting economic growth.

## 🧠 MNEMONICS & MEMORY TRICKS

*   **EFTA TEPA:** Remember the members with **NILS**: **N**orway, **I**celand, **L**iechtenstein, **S**witzerland. The deal involves **T**rade & **E**conomic **P**artnership **A**greement.
*   **DTTI (India-US Defence):** "US and India **D**efence **T**ies for **T**echnology **I**nnovation" – links the partners, purpose, and key area.
*   **LUPEX (ISRO-JAXA):** "**LU**nar **P**olar **EX**ploration by **I**ndia-**JA**pan".
*   **30x30 Target:** For the Kunming-Montreal GBF, remember "30% by 2030" for land and sea conservation.
*   **Gabriel Attal:** France's **Att**ractive and **Tall**est (youngest) PM.
*   **Sarnath UNESCO:** **S**arnath, **S**ermon, **S**ignificant, **S**outh Asia.

## ⚠️ EXAM TRAPS & COMMON MISTAKES

*   **Confusing Partners:** Be careful not to mix up which country is partnered with India for which specific defence or space collaboration (e.g., F414 with US, MCM with France, LUPEX with Japan).
*   **Specifics of Agreements:** Details like the $100 billion investment commitment in the EFTA TEPA or the 30x30 target for the GBF are potential MCQ points.
*   **Locational Awareness:** Knowing the host country/city for major international events (e.g., Bürgenstock, Switzerland for the Ukraine Peace Summit; Colombo, Sri Lanka for DIVEX).
*   **Nuance of Border Issues:** While the Galwan patrol reported no incidents, understanding the context of ongoing India-China LAC tensions is crucial.
*   **UN Structure:** Understanding the roles of the UN General Assembly and Security Council, and the terms of non-permanent members.
*   **Purpose of Initiatives:** Differentiate the core objectives of initiatives like DTTI (co-development) from general defence procurement.
*   **Cultural vs. Scientific:** Clearly distinguish between cultural diplomacy (Sarnath) and scientific diplomacy (LUPEX).

## 🔄 PYQ-STYLE PRACTICE QUESTIONS

1.  In March 2026, India signed a historic Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement (TEPA) with the European Free Trade Association (EFTA) bloc. Which of the following countries is NOT a member of the EFTA bloc?
    a) Norway
    b) Sweden
    c) Switzerland
    d) Iceland

    **Answer: b) Sweden**
    *Explanation: The EFTA bloc comprises Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, and Switzerland. Sweden is a member of the European Union, not EFTA.*

2.  The India-US Defence Technology and Trade Initiative (DTTI) held its annual dialogue in July 2026. What was a primary focus of the discussions during this initiative?
    a) Co-production of commercial aircraft and civilian technologies.
    b) Joint space exploration missions to Mars.
    c) Co-development and co-production of advanced defence systems.
    d) Bilateral trade agreements on agricultural products.

    **Answer: c) Co-development and co-production of advanced defence systems.**
    *Explanation: The DTTI is a bilateral forum specifically aimed at strengthening defence technology cooperation, including co-development and co-production, and technology transfer for advanced defence systems.*

3.  The Global Peace Summit on Ukraine, hosted in Bürgenstock, Switzerland, in June 2026, aimed to discuss which key aspects related to the conflict?
    a) Economic sanctions against nations involved in the conflict.
    b) The sovereignty and nuclear security aspects of Ukraine.
    c) The formation of a new international peacekeeping force.
    d) The establishment of a post-conflict reconstruction fund.

    **Answer: b) The sovereignty and nuclear security aspects of Ukraine.**
    *Explanation: The summit focused on addressing the foundational principles of the conflict, particularly Ukraine's sovereignty and the critical issue of nuclear security.*

4.  Which Indian Naval Ships concluded a port call at Port Victoria, Seychelles, in June 2026, engaging in professional, ceremonial, and community activities as part of India's maritime outreach?
    a) INS Vikrant and INS Kochi
    b) INS Kolkata and INS Chennai
    c) INS Tarkash and INS Ikshak
    d) INS Sahyadri and INS Shivalik

    **Answer: c) INS Tarkash and INS Ikshak**
    *Explanation: The raw event explicitly mentions INS Tarkash and Ikshak making port calls in Seychelles.*

5.  The ancient Buddhist site of Sarnath was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in July 2026. What is its profound historical and spiritual significance?
    a) It is the birthplace of Lord Buddha.
    b) It is where Lord Buddha attained enlightenment.
    c) It is the place where Lord Buddha delivered his first sermon.
    d) It is where Lord Buddha passed away.

    **Answer: c) It is the place where Lord Buddha delivered his first sermon.**
    *Explanation: Sarnath is revered as the deer park where Gautama Buddha first taught the Dharma (doctrine) to his five disciples after attaining enlightenment.*
