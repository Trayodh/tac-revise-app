*   **Confusing Acts:** Don't confuse the DPDP Act with older IT Acts or cybersecurity policies.
*   **Amendment Number:** A common trap is getting the Constitutional Amendment number for Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam wrong (it's 106th).
*   **CAA Specifics:** Misremembering the exact communities, countries, or the cut-off date (Dec 31, 2014) for CAA.
*   **WDS Location:** Forgetting that WDS 2026 was held in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.
*   **Exercise Type:** Confusing 'Tarang Shakti' (Air Force) with a Navy or Army exercise.
*   **Implementation Stages:** Overlooking that the Nari Shakti Adhiniyam's full implementation requires a census and delimitation.
