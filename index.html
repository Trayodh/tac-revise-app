Static Sports Facts & Terminologies

Olympic Games

  • Origin: Ancient Olympics held in Olympia, Greece (776 BC). Modern Olympics started in 1896 (Athens).
  • Governing Body: International Olympic Committee (IOC), headquartered in Lausanne, Switzerland.
  • Symbol: Five rings representing five inhabited continents: Blue (Europe), Yellow (Asia), Black (Africa), Green (Oceania), Red (Americas).
  • Motto: "Citius, Altius, Fortius – Communiter" (Faster, Higher, Stronger – Together).

Commonwealth & Asian Games

  • Commonwealth Games: First held in 1930 (Hamilton, Canada). Held every 4 years. Only nations from the Commonwealth participate.
  • Asian Games (Asiad): First held in 1951 (New Delhi, India). Organized by the Olympic Council of Asia (OCA).
  • Mascots: Both events heavily feature mascots representing the host nation's culture or wildlife.

Major Cups & Trophies

  • Football: Durand Cup (Asia's oldest tournament, Current Winner: Mohun Bagan SG), Santosh Trophy (Current: Services).
  • Cricket: Ranji Trophy (Current: Mumbai), Duleep Trophy, Irani Cup.
  • Badminton: Thomas Cup (Men's Team), Uber Cup (Women's Team).
  • Tennis: Davis Cup (Men's Team, Current: Italy), Billie Jean King Cup (Women's Team).
  • Hockey: Sultan Azlan Shah Cup, Aga Khan Cup.
