SENTENCE
IMPROVEMENT
Generally, 10 to 15 questions based on sentence improvement are asked in the CDS exam. In these
questions, a part of the given sentences is underlined. Out of the given options, the candidate has to choose
the option which improves the sentence. In case, there is no improvement, option (d) needs to be selected
which stands for 'No improvement'.
Questions based on Sentence Improvement are designed to test a candidate's knowledge of English
Grammar and Vocabulary simultaneously. In such questions, a sentence is given with a part underlined. For
this part, some alternatives are given below the sentence.
The candidate has to choose the option which improves the sentence. A sound knowledge of
grammatical rules is highly beneficial in solving such questions.
Direction (Example) Which option improves the underlined part of the given sentence? In case, the sentence
cannot be improved further, choose option (d).
The girl is wise despite her age.
(a) is her
(c) for her
(b) than her
(d) at her
Explanation (c) In the above sentence, underlined part is 'despite her'. We have to choose the option which
will improve the sentence. Among the given options, 'for her' is best option which improves the
sentence and makes it grammatically correct. The other options are incorrect. Hence, option (c) is
the correct choice.
