**Page 1: Banking and Inflation**

1. **What is Banking?**
- Banking refers to the provision of financial services such as acceptance of deposits, loaning, and other facilities to its customers.
- Types of Banks: Commercial Banks, Central Banks, Co-operative Banks, etc.
- Role of Banks:
    - Lending Money: Provides loans to the public.
    - Payment System: Acts as a medium of payment and facilitating the exchange of goods and services.
    - Store of Value: Safeguards deposits and makes them easily withdrawable on demand.

2. **What is Inflation?**
- Inflation is an increase in general price level in an economy over a period of time.
- Types of Inflation: Demand-Pull Inflation, Cost-Push Inflation, Built-in Inflation.
- Causes and Effects of Inflation:
    - Causes: Excess demand, Increase in Money Supply, External Pressure, etc.
    - Effects: Loss of Value of Money, Uneven Distribution of Wealth, etc.
    - Control Measures of Inflation: Monetary Policy, Fiscal Policy, Price and Income Policy, etc.

3. **Monetary Policy and Public Finance**
- **Monetary Policy:** A Central Bank's actions to control inflation and manage the money supply of an economy.
- **Public Finance:** The systematic study of the methods used by governments to raise revenue and allocate resources for public consumption and investment.

4. **Key Concepts**
- GNP (Gross National Product), National Income, GDP (Gross Domestic Product), NPA (Non-Performing Assets).
- Fiscal Deficit, Budgetary Deficit, Revenue Deficit, Primary Deficit.
