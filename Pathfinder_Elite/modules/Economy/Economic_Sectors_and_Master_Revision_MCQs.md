**Page 1: Economic Sectors - Overview**

**A. Primary Sector:**
1. Definition: The primary sector involves the extraction of raw materials from the earth (natural resources), such as agriculture, mining, and forestry.
2. Activities: Farming, fishing, hunting, logging, and extraction of mineral resources.
3. Characteristics:
 * Direct involvement with the natural environment
 * Raw materials are the product
4. Examples:
 * Agriculture (farmers growing crops)
 * Mining (extracting minerals)
 * Forestry (logging and timber harvesting)

**B. Secondary Sector:**
1. Definition: The secondary sector involves the processing of raw materials into finished goods, such as manufacturing and construction.
2. Activities: Production of goods through various processes, including assembly, fabrication, and assembly.
3. Characteristics:
 * Involves transformation of raw materials
 * Finished goods are the product
4. Examples:
 * Manufacturing (production of cars, electronics, etc.)
 * Construction (building bridges, houses, etc.)

**C. Tertiary Sector:**
1. Definition: The tertiary sector involves the provision of services, such as transportation, communication, and finance.
2. Activities: Provision of services to support the primary and secondary sectors.
3. Characteristics:
 * Does not involve the production of physical goods
 * Focuses on support and provision of services
4. Examples:
 * Transportation (delivery of goods and people)
 * Communication (telephones, internet, etc.)
 * Finance (banking, insurance, etc.)

**Answers to MCQs**

1. Which sector involves the extraction of raw materials from the earth?
A. Primary sector
B. Secondary sector
C. Tertiary sector
Answer: A. Primary sector

2. What is the primary function of the tertiary sector?
A. Production of physical goods
B. Provision of services to support the primary and secondary sectors
C. Extraction of raw materials from the earth
Answer: B. Provision of services to support the primary and secondary sectors

**Page 2: Primary Sector - Agriculture**

**Characteristics of Agriculture:**

1. Involves the cultivation of crops and the rearing of livestock
2. Raw materials are produced through the process of photosynthesis
3. Human labor is required for planting, harvesting, and other agricultural activities

**Advantages of Agriculture:**

1. Provides employment opportunities
2. Can be a source of income
3. Helps in the production of food grains and other essential crops

**Types of Agriculture:**

1. Subsistence farming: Focused on meeting the basic needs of the family, with minimal surplus.
2. Commercial farming: Focuses on producing surplus for sale or exchange.
3. Industrial farming: Large-scale farming with high-tech infrastructure.

**Examples of Agricultural Activities:**

1. Farming (production of crops)
2. Livestock production (rearing of animals for meat, dairy, and wool)
3. Horticulture (production of fruits, vegetables, flowers, etc.)

**Answers to MCQs**

1. What is the primary function of agriculture?
A. Production of finished goods
B. Extraction of raw materials from the earth
C. Provision of services to support the primary and secondary sectors
D. Cultivation of crops and rearing of livestock
Answer: D. Cultivation of crops and rearing of livestock

2. What type of farming focuses on producing surplus for sale or exchange?
A. Subsistence farming
B. Commercial farming
C. Industrial farming
Answer: B. Commercial farming

**Page 3: Economic Sectors - Industrialization and Services**

**Industrialization:**

1. Definition: The process of developing manufacturing and infrastructure to support industrial production.
2. Activities: Expansion of production and processing of raw materials and goods.
3. Characteristics:
 * Increased use of machinery and technology
 * Higher level of production and processing.

**Examples of Industrial Activities:**

1. Manufacturing (production of cars, electronics, etc.)
2. Construction (building bridges, houses, etc.)
3. Energy production (electricity, gas, etc.)

**Services Sector:**

1. Definition: The provision of services to support the primary and secondary sectors.
2. Activities: Provision of services to support the primary and secondary sectors.
3. Characteristics:
 * Does not involve the production of physical goods
 * Focuses on support and provision of services

**Examples of Service Activities:**

1. Transportation (delivery of goods and people)
2. Communication (telephones, internet, etc.)
3. Finance (banking, insurance, etc.)

**Answers to MCQs**

1. What is the key characteristic of industrialization?
A. Increased use of human labor
B. Increased use of machinery and technology
C. Higher level of production and processing
D. Reduced environmental impact
Answer: B. Increased use of machinery and technology

2. What type of sector provides services to support the primary and secondary sectors?
A. Primary sector
B. Secondary sector
C. Tertiary sector
Answer: C. Tertiary sector
