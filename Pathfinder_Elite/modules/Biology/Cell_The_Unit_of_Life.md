#### 1. The Structure of Cells
 Cells are the basic units of life. They are the smallest functional units of living organisms. All living cells have three basic components - a cell membrane, cytoplasm, and hereditary material.

#### 1.1 Cell Membrane
_The cell membrane, also known as the plasma membrane, is a thin semi-permeable membrane that separates the cell from the external environment. It regulates the movement of substances in and out of the cell through diffusion, osmosis, and active transport._

#### 1.2 Cytoplasm
_The cytoplasm is the jelly-like substance inside the cell membrane. It is composed of water, salts, sugars, and various organelles such as mitochondria, ribosomes, and lysosomes. Cytoplasm plays an essential role in cell metabolism and maintains cell shape and organization._

#### 1.3 Hereditary Material
_Hereditary material, also known as DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid), is found in the nucleus of eukaryotic cells. It contains genetic information that specifies the characteristics of an organism, including eye color, hair color, height, and other traits._

#### 1.4 Cell Divisions
_Cell division is the process by which a cell divides into two daughter cells. It occurs in two stages - mitosis and meiosis. Mitosis results in the formation of two identical daughter cells, while meiosis results in four non-identical sex cells (gametes)._


### High-Yield Concepts

* **Cellular Structure**: Cells are composed of a cell membrane, cytoplasm, and hereditary material, which regulate various cellular processes.
* **Cell Division**: Cell division occurs in two stages - mitosis and meiosis, resulting in the formation of daughter cells or gametes.
