**Biology: Animal Physiology Revision Notes**

**Page 1: Introduction to Animal Physiology**

1. **Definition of Animal Physiology**: The study of the structure and function of living organisms, focusing on the processes that occur within an animal's body.
2. **Importance of Animal Physiology**: Understanding animal physiology is crucial in veterinary medicine, animal breeding, and conservation biology.
3. **Scope of Animal Physiology**: Covers various topics, including movement, respiration, circulation, digestion, excretion, and regulation of body temperature, pH, and osmotic balance.

**Key Terms and Definitions**

- **Homeostasis**: The ability of an organism to maintain a stable internal environment despite changes in the external environment.
- **Negative feedback loop**: A mechanism that maintains homeostasis by counteracting changes in the internal environment.
- **Positive feedback loop**: A mechanism that amplifies changes in the internal environment, but usually leads to instability.

**Page 2: Movement and Locomotion**

1. **Types of Movement**: Voluntary movement, involuntary movement, and reflex movement.
2. **Muscular System**: Composition, structure, and functions of skeletal, smooth, and cardiac muscles.
3. **Movement of Organs and Body Systems**: Respiratory, circulatory, and digestive systems' movement.
4. **Locomotion**: Movement of an animal's body, using its muscles and skeleton.

**Key Terms and Definitions**

- **Proteins involved in muscle contraction**: Actin, myosin, and tropomyosin.
- **Types of motor neurons**: Alpha, beta, and gamma motor neurons.
- **Innervation of muscles**: The connection between motor neurons and muscles.

**Page 3: Respiration and Circulation**

1. **Respiration**: Process by which the body takes in oxygen and expels carbon dioxide.
2. **Circulation**: The process of transporting oxygen and nutrients to cells and removing waste products.

**Key Concepts**

- **Oxygen supply**: Oxygen diffuses from the air breathed in, into the bloodstream.
- **Transport of O2 and CO2**: Role of hemoglobin and Bohr effect.
- **Hemodynamics**: Pressure and flow of blood through the cardiovascular system.
- **Types of circulation**: Continuous, intermittent, and open.

**Page 4: Digestion, Excretion, and Regulation of Body Functions**

1. **Digestion**: Breakdown of food into smaller molecules for absorption.
2. **Excretion**: Removal of waste products from the body.
3. **Regulation of Body Functions**: Mechanisms that maintain homeostasis, such as negative feedback loops.

**Key Concepts**

- **Digestive system**: Mouth, esophagus, stomach, small intestine, and large intestine.
- **Types of digestion**: Mechanical and enzymatic digestion.
- **Regulation of pH**: The role of buffers, kidneys, and lungs.
- **Thermoregulation**: The control of body temperature.
