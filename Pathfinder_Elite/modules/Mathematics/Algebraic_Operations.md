**Page 1: Algebraic Operations Fundamentals**

* **Introduction**
  - Algebraic operations involve numbers and symbols manipulated using mathematical rules.
  - Basic operations include addition (+), subtraction (-), multiplication (×), and division (/).
* **Order of Operations**
  - When multiple operations are present, PEMDAS is used to resolve:
    1. **P**arentheses: Evaluate expressions inside parentheses first.
    2. **E**xponents: Evaluate any exponential expressions next.
    3. **M**ultiplication and **D**ivision: Evaluate these operations from left to right.
    4. **A**ddition and **S**ubtraction: Evaluate these operations from left to right.
* **Inverse Operations**
  - Inverse operations involve performing the opposite operation to cancel out the original:
    - Inverse of + is -
    - Inverse of - is +
    - Inverse of × is ÷
    - Inverse of ÷ is ×
* **Example**
  - Solve the expression: 3(2 + 5) × 4
  - Step 1: Evaluate expressions inside parentheses
  - 3(2 + 5) = 3(7)
  - Step 2: Simplify the expression inside the parentheses
  - = 3 × 7
  - Step 3: Perform multiplication
  - = 21
  - Step 4: Multiply the result by 4
  - 21 × 4 = 84

**Page 2: Algebraic Operations - Multiplication and Division**

* **Multiplication of Single-Digit Numbers**
  - When multiplying single-digit numbers:
    - Numbers can be multiplied in any order
    - Any number multiplied by 0 is 0
* **Division of Single-Digit Numbers**
  - When dividing single-digit numbers:
    - Divisors and quotients can be swapped
    - Any number divided by 0 is undefined
* **Multiplication and Division of Algebraic Expressions**
  - When multiplying or dividing algebraic expressions:
    - Distributive property: a(b + c) = ab + ac
    - Commutative property: a × b = b × a and a ÷ b = b ÷ a
* **Example**
  - Solve the equation: 2x × 3x = 12
  - Step 1: Apply the distributive property
  - 2x × 3x = 6x²
  - Step 2: Set the expression equal to 12
  - Step 3: Solve for x
  - 6x² = 12
  - ÷6: x² = 2

**Page 3: Algebraic Operations - Addition and Subtraction**

* **Addition of Single-Digit Numbers**
  - When adding single-digit numbers:
    - Numbers can be added in any order
    - Any number added to 0 is the number itself
* **Subtraction of Single-Digit Numbers**
  - When subtracting single-digit numbers:
    - Subtrahend must be smaller than the minuend
    - When the subtrahend is larger, the result is a negative number
* **Addition and Subtraction of Algebraic Expressions**
  - When adding or subtracting algebraic expressions:
    - Combine like terms
    - When combining different terms, apply the commutative property
* **Example**
  - Solve the expression: 4x + 2x - 3x
  - Step 1: Combine like terms
  - 4x + 2x - 3x = 3x
  - Result: 3x

**Page 4: Algebraic Operations - Identities and Inequalities**

* **Identities**
  - Algebraic identities are true for all values of the variables:
    - a × a = a²
    - a + (-a) = 0
* **Inequalities**
  - Inequalities involve comparing variables or expressions:
    - When solving inequalities, the opposite operation is involved:
      - If a > b, then a - b > 0
      - If a < b, then a - b < 0
* **Solving Linear Inequalities**
  - When solving linear inequalities:
    - Isolate the variable on one side of the inequality
    - Flip the inequality sign when multiplying or dividing both sides by a negative number
* **Example**
  - Solve the inequality: 3x < 12
  - Step 1: Divide both sides by 3
  - Step 2: Solve for x
  - x < 4
