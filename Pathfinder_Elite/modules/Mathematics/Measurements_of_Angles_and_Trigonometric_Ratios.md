ANGLE An angle is considered as the figure obtained by rotating a given ray about its endpoint. The original ray is called the initial side and the ray into which the initial side rotates is called terminal side. In figure, OA is initial side and OB is the terminal side of ∠AOB. The point O is called its vertex.
