**Revision Notes: Measurements of Angles and Trigonometric Ratios**

**Page 1: Introduction to Angles**

- **Definition of Angles**: An angle is defined as the figure formed by two rays that share a common endpoint, known as the vertex.
- **Angle Properties**: Angles can be acute, right, obtuse, straight or reflex, based on their measure.
- **Measure of an Angle**: The measure of an angle is the amount of rotation required to bring one of its arms to the other arm.
- **Standard Unit of Measurement**: The standard unit for measuring angles is degrees.

**Key Terms**

- Acute Angle: An angle with a measure between 0° and 90°.
- Right Angle: An angle with a measure of exactly 90°.
- Obtuse Angle: An angle with a measure between 90° and 180°.
- Straight Angle: An angle with a measure of exactly 180°.
- Reflex Angle: An angle with a measure between 180° and 360°.

**Page 1 Key Formula**

- Sum of the Measures of Interior Angles in a Triangle: 180° (S + T + U = 180°)
- Measure of an Exterior Angle: Equal to the sum of the measures of the remote interior angles (A + B = C, if E is an exterior angle)

**Page 2: Trigonometric Ratios**

- **Definition of Trigonometric Ratios**: Trigonometric ratios are used to calculate the unknown sides and angles in a triangle. The three basic ratios are sine, cosine and tangent.
- **Sine Function**: The sine of an angle is the ratio of the length of the opposite side to the hypotenuse (sin A = opposite / hypotenuse)
- **Cosine Function**: The cosine of an angle is the ratio of adjacent side to hypotenuse (cos A = adjacent / hypotenuse)
- **Tangent Function**: The tangent of an angle is the ratio of opposite side to adjacent side (tan A = opposite / adjacent)

**Key Formulas**

- Pythagorean Identity: sin^2(A) + cos^2(A) = 1 (sin^2A + cos^2A = 1)
- Trigonometric Ratios in Right Triangle: sin(A) = opposite / hypotenuse, cos(A) = adjacent / hypotenuse, tan(A) = opposite / adjacent

**Page 3: Important Relations and Formulas**

- **Sum of Exterior Angles in a Polygon**: The sum of the measures of the exterior angles of a polygon is equal to 360°
- **Measure of a Central Angle**: The measure of a central angle is equal to the measure of the intercepted arc
- **Relationship between Central Angle and Inscribed Angle**: The measure of an inscribed angle is half the measure of the central angle that intercepts the same arc

- Central Angle and Inscribed Angle: ∠(Central Angle)/ 2 = ∠(Inscribed Angle)
- Sum of Exterior Angles of Polygon: 360°
