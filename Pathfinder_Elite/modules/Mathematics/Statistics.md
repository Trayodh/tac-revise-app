**Page 1: Simple Interest and Compound Interest**

**1.1 Definition**

- Simple Interest (SI): The interest paid when interest is calculated only on the initial principal amount.
- Compound Interest (CI): The interest paid when interest is calculated on both the principal amount and any accrued interest.

**1.2 Formulae**

- Simple Interest: SI = (P × R × T)/100
- Compound Interest: A = P(1 + R/100)^T

**1.3 Example 1 (Source 1)**

- Given: SI = `400, R = 10%, T = 1 month = 1/12 year
- Solve for P: P × R × T = SI × 100
- P = (SI × 100) / (R × T) = (`400 × 100) / (10 × 1/12) = `48000
- Verify: SI = (`48000 × 10 × 1/12) / 100 = `400 (given)

**1.4 Example 2 (Source 1)**

- Given: P = `400, R = 10%, T = ?
- Required to find: T when SI is equal to the simple interest on `1000 for 4 years at 4% per annum
- Simple interest on `1000 at 4% for 4 years: (1000 × 4 × 4) / 100 = `160
- Now, simple interest = `160, P = `400, R = 10%
- SI = (P × R × T) / 100 = (`160 × 100) / (400 × 10) = 0.4 years

**Page 2: Statistics and Graphs**

**2.1 Types of Graphs**

1. **Bar Graph**: a graphical representation of data using bars of different heights or lengths
2. **Pie Chart**: a circular graph used to show the proportion of different categories
3. **Line Graph**: a graphical representation of data using a continuous line
4. **Histogram**: a graphical representation of data using horizontal or vertical bars

**2.2 Measures of Central Tendency**

1. **Mean**: the average value of a dataset
 Formula: (Σx) / N
2. **Median**: the middle value of a dataset when arranged in order
 Formula: n/2th term for an even number of terms
3. **Mode**: the value that appears most frequently in a dataset

**2.3 Example**

- Consider the following dataset: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10
- Mean = (2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10) / 5 = 30/5 = 6
- Median = (4 + 6) / 2 = 5 (midpoint of the two middle terms)
- Mode = 6 (appears most frequently)

**Page 3: Probability and Sets**

**3.1 Venn Diagrams**

- A Venn diagram is a graphical representation of sets and their relationships
- It consists of overlapping circles representing different sets

**3.2 Union and Intersection of Sets**

- **Union**: the set of all elements in either set
 Formula: A ∪ B = {a ∈ A | a ∈ B}
- **Intersection**: the set of elements common to both sets
 Formula: A ∩ B = {a ∈ A | a ∈ B}

**3.3 Example**

- Let A = {1, 2, 3, 4} and B = {3, 4, 5, 6}
- Union: A ∪ B = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}
- Intersection: A ∩ B = {3, 4}

**Page 4: Random Variables and Discrete Distributions**

**4.1 Random Variables**

- A random variable is a quantity that depends on chance or probability
- It can be either discrete or continuous

**4.2 Discrete Distributions**

- **Binomial Distribution**: a discrete distribution describing the probability of getting exactly k successes in n independent trials
 Formula: P(X = k) = (nCk) × p^k × q^(n-k)
- **Poisson Distribution**: a discrete distribution describing the probability of getting exactly k events in a fixed interval
 Formula: P(X = k) = e^(-λ) × (λ^k) / k!

**4.3 Example**

- A coin is tossed 5 times. What is the probability of getting exactly 3 heads?
- Binomial distribution: P(X = 3) = (5C3) × (1/2)^3 × (1/2)^(5-3)
= (10) × (1/2)^5
= 0.3125
