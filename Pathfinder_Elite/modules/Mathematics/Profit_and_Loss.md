**Page 1: Introduction to Profit and Loss**

1. **Definition of Profit and Loss**
   - Profit: The gain or excess of revenue over the cost incurred.
   - Loss: The excess of cost over the revenue gained.

2. **Types of Profit and Loss**
   - **Realized Profit/Loss**: The profit or loss realized on the sale of an asset.
     - Realized profit/loss = Selling price - Cost price.
   - **Nominal Profit/Loss**: The profit or loss arising from the sale of goods/services.
     - Nominal profit/loss = Revenue - Cost price of goods/ services.

3. **Basic Formulas**
   - **Profit** = Selling price - Cost price
   - **Loss** = Cost price - Selling price

**Page 2: Calculating Profit and Loss**

1. **Calculating Revenue and Cost**
   - Revenue = Selling price × Quantity sold
   - Cost price = Cost price of goods/services per unit × Quantity sold

2. **Formula for Profit**
   - Profit = Revenue - Cost price
   - Profit percentage = (Profit / CP) × 100

3. **Formula for Loss**
   - Loss = Cost price - Revenue
   - Loss percentage = (Loss / CP) × 100

4. **Examples and Practice Questions**
   - Example 1: Calculate the profit or loss when selling 1000 pens at ₹120 per pen, and the cost price is ₹100 per pen.
   - Example 2: Calculate the loss percentage when selling books at ₹150 each, and the cost price is ₹180 per book. 100 books are sold.

**Page 3: Applications of Profit and Loss**

1. **Discount and Markup**
   - **Discount**: Reduction in price.
   - **Markup**: Increase in price.

2. **Trade Discounts**
   - **Cash Discount**: When payment is made immediately.
   - **Trade Discount**: When sale is made to retailers.

3. **Percentage Calculations**
   - **Discount Percentage** = (Discount amount / Marked price) × 100
   - **Markup Percentage** = (Markup amount / Marked price) × 100

4. **Examples and Practice Questions**
   - Example 3: Calculate the marked price when a book is sold for ₹600 with a discount of 20%.
   - Example 4: Calculate the selling price when a shirt is marked up by 25%.
