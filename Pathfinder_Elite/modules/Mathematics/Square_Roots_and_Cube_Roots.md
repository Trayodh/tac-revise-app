# Chapter Overview: _Square Roots and Cube Roots_

## 1.1 Definition and Significance

In mathematics, roots of a number are the values that, when multiplied by themselves a specified number of times, yield the original value. Square roots, in particular, are the values that, when multiplied by themselves, yield the original number. Cube roots, on the other hand, are the values that, when cubed, yield the original number. Understanding square roots and cube roots is crucial in solving mathematical problems, particularly in algebra and geometry.

## 1.2 Mechanics of Square Roots

The square root of a number is denoted by the symbol √ and represents the value that, when multiplied by itself, yields the original number. For example, the square root of 16 is 4, denoted as √16 = 4, because 4 × 4 = 16. This concept is essential in solving equations involving square roots.

## 1.3 Mechanics of Cube Roots

The cube root of a number is denoted by the symbol ∛ and represents the value that, when cubed, yields the original number. For example, the cube root of 27 is 3, denoted as ∛27 = 3, because 3 × 3 × 3 = 27. This concept is essential in solving equations involving cube roots.

### 1.3.1 Properties of Square Roots

* The square root of a number is always non-negative.
* The square root of a negative number is undefined in the real number system.
* The square root of a perfect square is an integer.

### 1.3.2 Properties of Cube Roots

* The cube root of a number is always non-negative.
* The cube root of a negative number is negative.
* The cube root of a perfect cube is an integer.

## 1.4 Applications of Square Roots and Cube Roots

* Solve equations involving square roots and cube roots.
* Find the length of the sides of a square or cube given the area or volume.
* Find the volume of a cube or rectangular pyramid given the length of the sides.
* Solve problems involving periodic phenomena, such as sound waves and light waves.

### 1.4.1 Example of Applying Square Roots and Cube Roots

To find the length of the side of a square with an area of 16, we use the formula:

side = √area

side = √16
side = 4

Similarly, to find the length of the side of a cube with a volume of 27, we use the formula:

side = ∛volume

side = ∛27
side = 3

### Practice Question 1

Find the square root of 16.

Solution:

We can rewrite 16 as the product of two identical numbers: 4 × 4.

Therefore:

$$\sqrt{16} = \sqrt{4 \times 4}$$

Applying the property $a\sqrt{b} = \sqrt{ab}$, we get:

$$\sqrt{16} = \sqrt{{(4)}^{2}}$$

Which is equal to:

$$\sqrt{16} = 4$$
