**Revision Notes: Area and Perimeter of Plane Figures**

**Page 1: Introduction to Plane Figures**

1. **Definition**: A plane figure is a two-dimensional shape that lies flat on a plane surface.
2. **Types of Plane Figures**: 
    - **Regular Polygons**: A shape with equal sides and equal interior angles.
    - **Irregular Polygons**: A shape with unequal sides and/or unequal interior angles.
3. **Properties of Plane Figures**: 
    - **Perimeter**: The total length of the sides of a plane figure.
    - **Area**: The amount of space enclosed by a plane figure.

**Page 2: Area and Perimeter Formulas**

1. **Polygon Formulas**:
    - **Area (A)**: A = (½) × (sum of all lengths) × (height or apothem)
    - **Perimeter (P)**: P = (sum of all lengths)
2. **Special Case Formulas**:
    - **Rectangle**: A = length × width; P = 2(length + width)
    - **Triangle**: A = (½) × base × height; P = a + b + c (a, b, c are side lengths)

**Example 1: Calculate the Area and Perimeter of a Rectangle**

Given: Length = 8 cm, Width = 5 cm
A = length × width = 40 cm²
P = 2(length + width) = 2(8 + 5) = 26 cm

**Example 2: Calculate the Area of a Triangle**

Given: Base = 6 cm, Height = 8 cm
A = (½) × base × height = (½) × 6 × 8 = 24 cm²

**Page 3: Real-World Applications of Plane Figures**

1. **Architecture**: Plane figures are used to design buildings and structures.
2. **Engineering**: Plane figures are used to design machines and mechanisms.
3. **Art**: Plane figures are used to create patterns and designs.

**Problem 1: An architect wants to design a rectangular room with an area of 100 m². If the length is 10 m, what is the width?**

Solution: A = length × width; 100 = 10 × width
 Width = 100/10 = 10 m

**Problem 2: A triangle with sides 3 cm, 4 cm, and 5 cm is created. What is its area?**

Solution: A = (½) × base × height
Using Heron's formula to find the height: A = 7.5 cm²
