**Mathematics Revision Notes: Compound Interest**

**Page 1: Introduction and Formulae**

* Compound Interest (CI) is the interest calculated on the initial principal and the accumulated interest of previous periods.
* The compound interest formula is A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt), where:
	+ A = amount after t years
	+ P = principal amount
	+ r = annual interest rate
	+ n = number of times interest is compounded per year
	+ t = time in years
* Simple Interest (SI) can be calculated using the formula SI = (P * r * t) / 100

**Page 2: Examples and Applications**

* Example 1:
	+ P = $1000
	+ r = 5% per annum
	+ n = 2 times per year (compounded semi-annually)
	+ t = 2 years
	+ Calculate the amount after 2 years
	+ A = 1000(1 + 0.05/2)^(2*2)
	+ A = $1102.51
* Applications:
	+ Compound interest is used by banks and financial institutions to calculate interest on savings accounts and investments.
	+ It's also used in personal finance to calculate the growth of investments over time.

**Page 3: Formulae and Derivations**

* Derivation of Compound Interest formula:
	+ A = P + SI for the first year
	+ A = P + SI + SI for the second year (accumulated interest is added to the principal)
	+ A = P(1 + r) + P(r/2)(1 + r)^(-1) + ... + P(r/n)(1 + r)^(-n) for n compounding periods
	+ After simplification, the formula becomes A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)
* Derivation of Simple Interest formula:
	+ SI = P * r * t can be derived from the compound interest formula by taking the limit as n approaches infinity.
