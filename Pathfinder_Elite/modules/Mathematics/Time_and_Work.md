**Page 1: Time and Work - Formulas and Relationships**

1. **Time and Work Formula:**
   - Work = (Efficiency × Time) / (Number of workers)
   - 1/ Work = Number of workers / Efficiency
   - Speed = Work / Time = Distance / Time
   - Time = Work / Speed
   - 1/ work = 1/ speed + 1/ time

2. **Relationships between Time, Work and Speed:**
   - If speed is constant, Work ∝ Time
   - If work is constant, Time ∝ 1/Speed
   - If work is constant, Speed ∝ 1/ Time

3. **Pythagoras Theorem and Triangles:**
   - For a right-angled triangle with legs AB = c, AC = d, and hypotenuse BC = a, then
     a^2 = c^2 + d^2 (Pythagoras Theorem)
   - For similar triangles, the corresponding sides are proportional
     (Area of ∆ABC) / (Area of ∆DEF) = ((height of ∆ABC)^2) / ((height of ∆DEF)^2)

1. **Solution to Problem:** Problem from Source 1
   ABC is a triangle right angled at A, and AD is perpendicular to BC. If BD = 8 cm and DC = 12.5 cm, then what is AD equal to?
   - Applying Pythagoras Theorem in triangle ADB:
     AD^2 + BD^2 = AB^2
   - Since triangle ADB is similar to triangle ADC, we can set a proportional relationship between their sides:
     (BD / AD)^2 = (AD / DC)^2
   - Applying the formula and substituting the values:
     (8 / AD)^2 = (AD / 12.5)^2
     8^2 / AD^2 = AD^2 / 12.5^2
   - Solving for AD:
     AD^2 / (8^2 * 12.5^2) = 1
     AD^2 = 12.5 * 8 * 8 / 8
     AD = 10 cm
   - Answer: 10 cm (d)

2. **Example Question:**
   - A group of 6 boys are working together to complete a project in 12 days. If one boy is replaced by a girl who is 25% efficient than the boy, how long will it take for the girls and boys (in their original ratio) to complete the same project?
   - Work done by the girl in 1 day = 125% of the work done by the boy in 1 day
   - Let the work done by the boy in 1 day = x
   - Work done by the girl in 1 day = 125% of x = 1.25x

**Page 3: Time and Work - Time and Work Problems with Multiple Steps**

1. **Example Question:**
   - 3 men and 4 women can complete a 6-part work in 6 days. How many days will it take for 2 men and 7 women to do the same work if the women are 25% more efficient than the men?
   - The work done per day by one man = x
   - The work done per day by one woman = 125% of x = 1.25x
   - Men's work = 3x
   - Total work per day by 3 men and 4 women = (3x) + (5 × 1.25x) = 3x + 6.25x = 9.25x
   - Total work per day by 2 men and 7 women = (2x) + (8.75x) = 10.75x
