**Page 1: Overview of Atomic Structure**

- **Atomic Structure:** Atomic structure refers to the arrangement and characteristics of the protons, neutrons, and electrons that constitute an atom.
- **History of Atomic Structure:** Discovery of subatomic particles, J.J. Thomson's cathode ray tube experiments, and Ernest Rutherford's gold foil experiment.
- **Key Concepts:**
  - Protons: Positively charged particles in the nucleus
  - Neutrons: Particles with no charge in the nucleus
  - Electrons: Negatively charged particles outside the nucleus
  - Atomic Number (Z): Number of protons in an atom's nucleus
  - Mass Number (A): Total number of protons and neutrons in an atom's nucleus

**Page 2: Electron Configuration and Orbitals**

- **Electron Configuration:** The arrangement of electrons in an atom's orbitals.
- **Electron Shells and Subshells:**
  - Electron shells: Energy levels surrounding the nucleus
  - Subshells: Partitions within each energy level (s, p, d, f)
- **Orbitals:** Regions where electrons are likely to be found.
- **Aufbau Principle and Pauli's Exclusion Principle:**
  - Aufbau Principle: Electrons fill lowest-energy orbitals first
  - Pauli's Exclusion Principle: Each orbital can hold a maximum of two electrons with opposite spins
- **Key Concepts:**
  - s-orbitals: Shape like spheres, occupy axial symmetry
  - p-orbitals: Shape like dumbbells, occupy axial symmetry

**Page 3: Atomic Models and Quantum Mechanics**

- **Atomic Models:**
  - Rutherford's Nuclear Model: Positively charged nucleus surrounded by negatively charged electrons
  - Thomson's Plum Pudding Model: Electrons are scattered throughout the atom
  - Bohr Model: Electrons occupy specific energy levels (shells) around the nucleus
- **Quantum Mechanics:**
  - Wave-particle duality: Electrons exhibit both wave-like and particle-like behavior
  - Uncertainty Principle: It's impossible to know both position and momentum of an electron simultaneously

**Page 4: Applications of Atomic Structure and Key Concepts**

- **Applications of Atomic Structure:**
  - Understanding chemical bonding
  - Predicting chemical reactivity
  - Understanding periodic trends
- **Key Concepts:**
  - Isotopes: Atoms with the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons
  - Radioactivity: Nuclei undergo radioactive decay
  - Electron affinity: Energy released when an electron is added to an atom
  - Ionization energy: Energy required to remove an electron from an atom
