**Inorganic Chemistry Revision Notes**

**Page 1: Introduction to Solid State Chemistry**

1. **Crystal Systems**
   - 3 main systems: isometric, tetragonal, orthorhombic
   - Key characteristics:
     - Isometric: all sides equal, all angles 90°
     - Tetragonal: sides a and b equal, angle γ = 90°
     - Orthorhombic: all sides unequal, all angles 90°

2. **Unit Cell**
   - Smallest repeated unit in crystal structure
   - 3 types:
     - Primitive: no lattice points in the middle of edges or faces
     - Face-centered: lattice points in each face
     - Body-centered: lattice point at the center

3. **Surface Area of Solids**
   - **Cuboid:** (2lw + 2lh + 2wh)
   - Example: surface area calculation
     - For a cuboid with dimensions: length = 12, width = 5, height = 5 (in units)
     (2lw + 2lh + 2wh) = (2 × 12 × 5 + 2 × 12 × 5 + 2 × 5 × 5) = 120 + 120 + 50 = 290 units
   - **Sphere:** 4πr^2
   - Example: surface area calculation
     - For a sphere with radius r = 4 cm
     4 × π × (4)^2 = 4 × π × 16 = 201.06 sq cm

**Page 2: Structure of Solids and Properties**

1. **Atomic Radius**
   - Definition: half the distance between nuclei of two adjacent atoms
   - Trend: increases down a group, decreases across a period

2. **Crystal Field Theory**
   - Splitting of d-orbitals in transition metal complexes
   - Factors influencing crystal field splitting constant (Δ):
     - Nature of metal
     - Coordination number
     - Charge
     - Cation size
