**Page 1: Grammar and Sentence Structure**

1. **Identifying Error Types**
    - Spelling mistakes (homophones, homographs)
    - Incorrect subject-verb agreement
    - Misused articles (a/an/the)
    - Verb tense inconsistencies
    - Comma and punctuation errors

**Error Analysis Techniques**

- Check for consistency in verb forms (e.g. present, past, past perfect)
- Identify and analyze sentence structures (simple, compound, complex)
- Be mindful of pronoun usage (subject, object, possessive)
- Use context clues to infer meaning from unclear or ambiguous sentences
