### Important Formulas and Identities

- **sin^2(A) + cos^2(A) = 1**
- **cosec(A) = 1/sin(A), sec(A) = 1/cos(A), cot(A) = 1/tan(A)**
- **tan(A) = sin(A)/cos(A)**
