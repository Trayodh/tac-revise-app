**Page 3: Comprehension Techniques**

* **SQ3R Technique:**
	+ Survey the passage to identify key details.
	+ Question what information is provided in the passage.
	+ Read the passage to identify supporting evidence.
	+ Recite the key points in the passage.
	+ Review the passage to reinforce understanding.
* **KWL Technique:**
	+ Know what you want to learn from the passage.
	+ What do you already know about the topic?
	+ Learn what new information the passage provides.
* **Summarization:**
	+ Summarize the main idea of the passage in your own words.
	+ Identify key points and supporting evidence.

**Example:**

* The SQ3R technique can be applied to the passage in Source 1 by surveying the diagram and identifying key details about the triangles, questioning the relationship between XY and PQ, reading the passage to identify supporting evidence, reciting the key points, and reviewing the passage to reinforce understanding.
