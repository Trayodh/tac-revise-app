**Part 1: Word Meanings and Sentence Examples**

1. **Decrease**
- Meaning: to become smaller in amount or quantity
- Example sentence: The company aims to decrease its carbon footprint.

2. **Increase**
- Meaning: to become larger in amount or quantity
- Example sentence: The population is expected to increase over the next decade.

3. **Deceased**
- Meaning: no longer alive
- Example sentence: She paid her respects to the deceased at the funeral.

4. **Rectangle**
- Meaning: a four-sided figure with four right angles
- Example sentence: The rectangle has a length of 5 cm and a breadth of 2 cm.

5. **Breadth**
- Meaning: the distance from one side of something to the other
- Example sentence: The width of the road is 4 meters and the breadth is 6 meters.

**Part 4: Word Origins and Synonyms**

1. **Rectangle**: Originated from the Latin words 'rectus' (straight) and 'angulus' (angle). Synonym: square

2. **Breadth**: Originated from the Old English words 'broad' and 'th' (dimension). Synonym: width

3. **Decrease**: Originated from the Latin word 'deducere' (to lead away). Synonym: reduction

4. **Increase**: Originated from the Latin word 'increare' (to increase or augment). Synonym: growth

**Part 11: Idioms and Phrases**

1. **To cut down**: to reduce the amount of something
Example sentence: We need to cut down our expenses to stay within budget.

2. **To boost**: to increase something
Example sentence: The new policy will boost the economy.

3. **To trim the fat**: to reduce waste or unnecessary expenses
Example sentence: The company needs to trim the fat to stay competitive.

4. **To turn up**: to increase
Example sentence: We need to turn up the heat in this cold room.
