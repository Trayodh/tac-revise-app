**Page 1: Introduction to Oscillations and Waves**

- **Definition**: Oscillations are repetitive back-and-forth motion of an object about a fixed point. Waves are disturbance that transfer energy through a medium.
- **Types of Oscillations**:
  - **Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)**: motion of an object attached to a spring or a pendulum.
  - **Damped Oscillations**: when the amplitude decreases due to external forces (friction, air resistance).
  - **Forced Oscillations**: oscillations driven by an external force.
- **Key Concepts**:
  - **Position Function (x)**: describes the motion of the object as a function of time.
  - **Velocity Function (v)**: describes the velocity of the object as a function of time.
  - **Acceleration Function (a)**: describes the acceleration of the object as a function of time.

**Page 2: Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)**

- **Definition**: motion of an object attached to a spring or a pendulum.
- **Key Characteristics**:
  - **Symmetry**: SHM is a symmetric motion about the equilibrium point.
  - **Periodic**: SHM is a periodic motion, meaning it repeats itself after a fixed time period (T).
  - **Energy Conservation**: energy is conserved in SHM, with kinetic energy and potential energy varying as the object moves.
- **Equations**:
  - **Position Function**: x(t) = x0\*cos(ωt + φ)
  - **Velocity Function**: v(t) = -x0\*ω\*sin(ωt + φ)
  - **Acceleration Function**: a(t) = -x0\*ω2\*cos(ωt + φ)

**Page 3: Types of Waves**

- **Types of Waves**:
  - **Travelling Waves**: wave propagates through a medium with a fixed velocity.
  - **Standing Waves**: wave appears to be stationary, with nodes and antinodes where the amplitude is zero.
- **Wave Characteristics**:
  - **Wavelength (λ)**: distance between two consecutive points on a wave with the same phase.
  - **Frequency (f)**: number of oscillations per second.
  - **Speed (v)**: distance traveled by a wave per second.
- **Equations**:
  - **Wave Velocity**: v = λf
  - **Wave Equation**: y(x, t) = A\*sin(kx - ωt)

**Page 4: Wave Propagation and Interference**

- **Wave Propagation**:
  - **Wave Speed**: depends on the properties of the medium (density, elasticity).
  - **Wave Reflection**: wave bounces back when it hits a barrier or an obstacle.
- **Interference**:
  - **Constructive Interference**: wave amplitudes add up, resulting in increased amplitude.
  - **Destructive Interference**: wave amplitudes cancel out, resulting in reduced amplitude.
- **Superposition Principle**: the sum of two or more waves at a point is equal to the sum of their individual contributions at that point.
