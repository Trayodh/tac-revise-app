**Revision Notes for Physics: Rotational Motion and Gravitation**

**Page 1: Rotational Motion**

### 1. Introduction to Rotational Motion

* Rotational motion refers to the circular or curved path traced by an object around a fixed point or axis.
* The study of rotational motion involves understanding the relationship between the object's motion and its angular displacement, angular velocity, and angular acceleration.

### 2. Rotational Kinematics

* **Angular Displacement (θ)**: The angle through which the object has rotated from its initial position.
* **Angular Velocity (ω)**: The rate of change of angular displacement, usually measured in radians per second (rad/s).
* **Angular Acceleration (α)**: The rate of change of angular velocity, usually measured in radians per second squared (rad/s²).

### 3. Rotational Kinematics Equations

* **Equation of Angular Displacement**: θ = ωt + ∅
* **Equation of Angular Velocity**: ω = ω₀ + αt
* **Equation of Angular Acceleration**: α = Δω / Δt

### 4. Types of Rotational Motion

* **Uniform Rotational Motion**: The object rotates at a constant angular velocity.
* **Non-Uniform Rotational Motion**: The object accelerates or decelerates during its rotation.

**Page 2: Gravitation**

### 1. Introduction to Gravitation

* Gravitation is a fundamental force of nature that attracts two objects with mass towards each other.
* The study of gravitation involves understanding the principles of gravitational force, gravitational field, and gravitational potential energy.

### 2. Gravitational Force

* **Gravitational Force (Fg)**: The force of attraction between two objects with mass, usually measured in newtons (N).
* **Gravitational Field (g)**: The region around an object where the force of gravitation can be detected.
* **Gravitational Potential Energy (Ug)**: The energy an object has due to its position in a gravitational field.

### 3. Gravitational Force Equations

* **Equation of Gravitational Force**: Fg = G \* (m₁ \* m₂) / r²
* **Equation of Gravitational Field**: g = G \* M / r²
* **Equation of Gravitational Potential Energy**: Ug = G \* (m₁ \* m₂) / r

### 4. Gravitational Field and Potential Energy

* **Gravitational Field Lines**: Imaginary lines that represent the direction and magnitude of the gravitational force at a point in space.
* **Gravitational Potential Energy Surface**: A graphical representation of the gravitational potential energy of an object at different positions.

**Page 3: Rotational Motion - Kinetics**

### 1. Rotational Kinetics

* Rotational kinetics involves the study of the relationship between an object's rotational motion and its torque, moment of inertia, and energy.

### 2. Torque

* **Torque (τ)**: A measure of the rotational force that causes an object to rotate, usually measured in newton-meters (N·m).
* **Equation of Torque**: τ = r \* F
* **Equation of Rotational Work**: W = τ \* θ

### 3. Moment of Inertia

* **Moment of Inertia (I)**: A measure of an object's resistance to changes in its rotational motion.
* **Equation of Moment of Inertia**: I = m \* r²
* **Equation of Rotational Kinetic Energy**: Ek = (1/2) \* I \* ω²

### 4. Rotational Dynamics

* **Rotational Dynamics**: The study of the relationship between an object's rotational motion and its external forces and torques.

**Page 4: Rotational Motion - Energy and Momentum**

### 1. Rotational Energy

* **Rotational Kinetic Energy (Ek)**: The energy an object has due to its rotational motion.
* **Equation of Rotational Kinetic Energy**: Ek = (1/2) \* I \* ω²
* **Equation of Rotational Potential Energy**: Ep = G \* (m₁ \* m₂) / r

### 2. Rotational Momentum

* **Angular Momentum (L)**: A measure of an object's tendency to continue rotating.
* **Equation of Rotational Momentum**: L = I \* ω
* **Equation of Conservation of Rotational Momentum**: L₁ + L₂ = L₁' + L₂'

### 3. Conservation of Rotational Energy

* **Conservation of Rotational Energy**: The total rotational energy of an isolated system remains constant over time.
* **Equation of Conservation of Rotational Energy**: Ek₁ + Ek₂ = Ek₁' + Ek₂'

### 4. Rotational Motion and Gravitational Waves

* **Gravitational Waves**: Ripples in the fabric of spacetime that are produced by the acceleration of massive objects.
* **Equation of Gravitational Waves**: h = (G \* E) / (c² \* r)
