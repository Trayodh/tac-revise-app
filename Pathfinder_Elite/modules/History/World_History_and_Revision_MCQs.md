**Page 1: Imperialism and Global Conflict**

**I. Introduction**

- Imperialism: Definition and effects on global conflict
- Rise of European powers and their colonies
- Competition for resources and territory

**II. European Imperialism (Late 19th - Early 20th century)**

- Britain: Dominance in the Indian subcontinent, Africa, and Asia
- France: Colonization of Africa and Southeast Asia
- Germany: Expansion in Africa and the Pacific
- United States: Imperial ambitions in the Caribbean and Pacific

**III. The Scramble for Africa (1881-1914)**

- Berlin Conference (1884-1885): European powers negotiate African colonization
- Impact on African societies and economies
- Role of European powers in exploiting African resources

**IV. Examples of Colonialism**

- India: British colonial rule and its effects on Indian society and economy
- Indonesia: Dutch colonial rule and its legacy

**Page 2: Global Conflict and World War I**

**I. Causes of World War I**

- Imperialism: Competition for resources and territory
- Nationalism: Rise of national identity and self-determination
- Militarism: Build-up of military power and aggression

**II. Major Events Leading to World War I**

- Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand (1914)
- Balkan wars (1912-1913)
- Alliance system: Interlocking military agreements between European powers

**III. World War I: Key Battles and Events**

- Battle of the Somme (1916)
- Battle of Verdun (1916)
- Entry of the United States into the war (1917)
- Russian Revolution and the withdrawal from the war

**IV. Long-term Consequences of World War I**

- Rise of the Soviet Union and communism
- Treaty of Versailles and its 'war guilt' clause
- Global economic instability and the onset of the Great Depression

**Page 3: Global Conflict and World War II**

**I. Causes of World War II**

- Unresolved issues from World War I
- Appeasement policy: Britain and France's policy of giving in to Nazi demands
- Rise of fascist and nationalist ideologies

**II. Major Events Leading to World War II**

- Invasion of Poland (1939)
- Soviet-Nazi Pact (1939)
- Battle of Britain (1940)
- Invasion of the Soviet Union (1941)

**III. World War II: Key Battles and Events**

- Battle of Stalingrad (1943)
- Battle of Normandy (1944)
- Atomic bombing of Hiroshima and Nagasaki (1945)
- Soviet capture of Berlin (1945)

**IV. Long-term Consequences of World War II**

- Rise of the United States and the Soviet Union as superpowers
- Emergence of the United Nations
- Division of Europe: Cold War and the Iron Curtain

**Page 4: Revision MCQs**

1. Which European power dominated the Indian subcontinent?
a) Britain
b) France
c) Germany
d) United States

Answer: a) Britain

2. Which event triggered the outbreak of World War I?
a) Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand
b) Battle of Verdun
c) Battle of the Somme
d) Treaty of Versailles

Answer: a) Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand

3. Which ideology was a key cause of World War II?
a) Communism
b) Fascism
c) Nationalism
d) All of the above

Answer: d) All of the above

4. What was the name of the treaty that imposed harsh penalties on Germany after World War I?
a) Treaty of Versailles
b) Treaty of Berlin
c) Treaty of Paris
d) Treaty of Versailles and Treaty of Berlin

Answer: a) Treaty of Versailles

5. Which battle marked the beginning of the end of the German resistance in World War II?
a) Battle of Stalingrad
b) Battle of Normandy
c) Battle of Berlin
d) Battle of El Alamein

Answer: a) Battle of Stalingrad
