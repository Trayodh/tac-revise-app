--- 2025-2026 INDIAN ARMED FORCES INDUCTION UPDATES ---
Naval Forces:
- INS Aridhaman (S4): India's third indigenous nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN) of the Arihant-class, commissioned early 2026.
- Project 17A Stealth Frigates: INS Taragiri, INS Mahendragiri, INS Dunagiri, INS Vindhyagiri (commissioning 2026).
- 2025 Inductions: 4th P15B destroyer (INS Surat), 1st P17A stealth frigate (INS Nilgiri), 6th P75 submarine (INS Vaghsheer), INS Arnala.

Air Force & Aircraft:
- LCA Mk-1A (Tejas): HAL delivering first squadrons by 2025-2026.
- C-295 Transport Aircraft: First locally assembled plane rolled out from Vadodara facility in 2026.

Missiles & Air Defense:
- Akash-NG: Next-generation surface-to-air missile inducted 2025-2026.
- Project Kusha: Long-range air defense missile system (S-400 equivalent) under induction/development.
- Strategic: Agni-4 and Agni-5 ballistic missiles operationalized further.
- Rampage missiles, Pralay ballistic missiles (inducted/ordered recently).
